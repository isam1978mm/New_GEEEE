# Aitik Mine cover-trial depth screen result

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

Aitik is a strong long-term stability near-miss, but the public papers do not provide final measured numerical depth labels or coordinate-controlled polygons.

## What the primary records prove

- Two large cover system trials were constructed on WRD6 in 2013.
- Each trial includes an approximately **50 m × 50 m plateau** and a 3H:1V sloped area.
- Both configurations use:
  - 0.3 m compacted till;
  - non-compacted protective till;
  - 0.3 m till-organic growth medium.
- The intended difference is:
  - Trial 1: 1.0 m protective till, approximately 1.6 m nominal total cover;
  - Trial 2: 1.5 m protective till, approximately 2.1 m nominal total cover.
- Monitoring continued for eleven years.
- Vegetation became well established.
- The 2025 paper reports no macro-scale erosion and performance consistent with the conceptual design.

## Fatal blocker

The 1.0 m and 1.5 m values are described as constructed design configurations. The public papers do not publish:

- final measured total thicknesses for each trial polygon;
- a thickness survey or pointwise before/after elevation table;
- numerical thickness tolerance or depth uncertainty;
- numerical horizontal or vertical survey accuracy;
- surveyed corner northings/eastings;
- a georeferenced GIS or CAD polygon deliverable.

The 2015 construction paper reports density, moisture and permeability testing of compacted layers, but those measurements do not establish the final total depth of the two broad trial zones.

The plateau dimensions are approximate, and monitoring infrastructure is present. Therefore, a 30-40 m clean interior cannot be drawn honestly from the public record without inventing boundaries or exclusions.
