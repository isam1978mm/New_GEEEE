The available public evidence also confirms ongoing infrastructure and operational confounders at 311 North Division Street:

- later mowing, excavating, and hauling work at the landfill property.

These facts do not automatically make every part of the cap unusable, but they prevent approval of a clean Sentinel-1 comparison area without the missing as-built map and a specific surface-stability review.

3. avoid gas wells, piping, drainage, leachate infrastructure, roads, and transfer-station activity;
4. determine a clean post-construction observation period;
5. calculate defensible geometry and uncertainty.
