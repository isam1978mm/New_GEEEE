# BDM Warren Steel pond-closure depth result - 2026-07-27

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

The BDM Warren Steel closure package documents completed closure of Pond 5 and the 56-inch Hot Mill Lagoon. Both areas were backfilled for positive drainage, received topsoil, and had vegetation established.

That gives useful same-surface closure evidence, but the package does not provide the final measured depth evidence required by the approved plan.

## Recovered closure evidence

The two former ponds had different internal backfill histories:

- Pond 5 received controlled fill containing mixed residuals and slag;
- the 56-inch Hot Mill Lagoon was backfilled without reusing the removed residuals.

Both areas were then topped with soil and vegetation.

## Fatal problem

The report section titled `As-Built Drawings for Waste-in Place Closures` states:

```text

```

The package does not provide:

- a final surface-to-buried-interface depth grid;
- as-built interface elevations for both ponds;
- certified shallow and deep polygons;

Different internal backfill materials do not create numeric depth labels by themselves. Without measured final interface depths, the two ponds cannot become a calibrated shallow/deep pair.
