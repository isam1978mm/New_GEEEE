## Confirmed in this pass

- The correct EPA remedial administrative-record file is Region 3 collection **AR206**, containing 71 documents.
- EPA's collection link is publicly listed, but the AR206 document table repeatedly failed to load through the available web and direct-access methods.
- The July 2001 construction-closeout document is inconsistently named in later EPA records:
  - some five-year reviews call it the **Remedial Action Completion Report**;
  - the 2008 Final Close Out Report calls it the **Remedial Action Construction Report**.
- EPA records place the report in July 2001 and show EPA approval on August 17, 2001.
- The second five-year review states that construction included:
  - about 7,000 feet of western-landfill inspection/access trails;
  - verification of western-landfill cover thickness;
  - eastern-landfill cap repair and verification of cover thickness.
- EPA, the Bureau of Reclamation, and PADEP performed construction oversight and concluded that the remedial activities were constructed according to the final design.
- The same review states that the completion report documented achievement of the ROD performance standards.
- Later inspections continued to describe the western landfill as forested, while identified repair issues focused mainly on eastern cap features, access roads, drainage corridors, and other discrete infrastructure.
- EPA records a fifth five-year review completed on July 2, 2025, but no retrievable copy was found in the visible EPA report list, SEMS indexing, Regulations.gov, or general web search.
- PDF screenshot attempts for the relevant EPA figures failed because the EPA cache could not retrieve the pages. No unseen geometry was used.
