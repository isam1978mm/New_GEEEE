# Berks Landfill Final Cap Evidence Result — 2026-07-27

## Decision

```text
NOT GOOD TO GO FOR THE CURRENT SENTINEL-1 DEPTH TEST
```

Berks Landfill has the right physical scale and a broadly comparable vegetated landfill surface. EPA's records confirm that the western landfill cover thickness was verified and that the eastern landfill received cap repairs, cover-thickness verification, revegetation, and erosion controls during the 2000 remedial action.

The public record does not publish the final measurements needed for calibration. The available collections contain the Preliminary Close Out Report and Five-Year Reviews, but no final construction report, post-repair point table, post-repair thickness-zone drawing, or numerical survey accuracy.

The earlier Record of Decision describes planning-era cover-thickness variation. Those pre-construction estimates cannot be substituted for final as-built depth values.

No Earth Engine query was run and no calibration row was created.
