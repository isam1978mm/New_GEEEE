# Depth Blocker 2 — California closure screen — 2026-07-21

Status: research only. No email, form, message, or records request was sent.

## Why California remains a high-value search path

California Title 27 requires an initial post-closure topographic survey/map after final closure and, where required, a five-year iso-settlement map tied to that baseline. In principle this is close to the desired matched-survey evidence package. The present screen tested recent named closures against the stricter calibration gate.

## Results

### Carpenter Road Landfill, Modesto — rejected

Official CEQA documentation describes the 2017 project as a clean-closure operation for the east landfill, with soil and waste excavated and relocated to the west landfill to create final grades and cover.

Decision: `rejected_material_relocation`

Reason: the event is not a simple final cap over waste left undisturbed in place. The test footprint would contain major excavation, hauling, relocation, and regrading, so any radar/topographic change could not be attributed to cap thickness or ordinary settlement.

Official source:
- California CEQAnet, Carpenter Road Landfill Clean Closure Project, SCH 2017022031: https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2017022031

### Ukiah Landfill — future watch

The City of Ukiah's official project page still describes the landfill closure as a proposed project and lists the required final-cover, grading, settlement accommodation, CQA, drainage, gas-control, and monitoring components. North Coast Water Board program priorities for FY 2026/27 still list preparation of revised/adopted closure waste-discharge requirements for the site.

Decision: `future_watch_not_completed_mature_pair`

Reason: a completed one-event final cap plus later five-year survey pair is not yet publicly demonstrated. No public closure certification/as-built surface and mature repeat-survey map were found in this screen.

Official sources:
- City of Ukiah, Ukiah Landfill Closure Project: https://cityofukiah.com/ukiah-landfill-closure-project/
- North Coast Regional Water Board, program priorities: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/northcoast/water_issues/programs/program_priorities/index.html

### South Coast Landfill, Mendocino County — future watch

Mendocino County published final-closure construction procurement in 2022–2023 and again in late 2025/early 2026. The current public record is therefore a construction/procurement trail, not a completed cap with a later repeat survey.

Decision: `future_watch_construction_stage`

Reason: no mature post-closure survey interval exists yet, and no public matched as-built plus repeat topographic surfaces were found.

Official sources:
- Mendocino County, South Coast Landfill Final Closure Construction Project, RFP 220001: https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/Home/Components/RFP/RFP/2111/385
- Mendocino County, South Coast Landfill Final Closure Construction Project, RFP 250042: https://www.mendocinocounty.gov/Home/Components/RFP/RFP/2437/

### Laytonville Landfill — rejected for this blocker

Official California and Mendocino County sources state that the landfill closed in 1993 and was capped in 1997. Current 2025–2026 work concerns cover remediation, repairs, and improvements after slope deficiencies were identified.

Decision: `rejected_pre_sentinel_original_cap_and_later_repairs`

Reason: the original cap predates Sentinel-1 by many years, while the current event is repair/remediation rather than a clean recent final-cap construction event. It cannot supply the desired recent cap-thickness calibration pair.

Official sources:
- California Water Boards tribal funding summary: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/tribal_affairs/funding.html
- Mendocino County File 25-0844, cover remediation and improvements project: https://mendocino.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=9FB4F61E-0BDD-48A4-87D2-C971BB19EAE2&ID=7690113&Options=&Search=
- Mendocino County File 26-0426, engineering/design for cover repairs: https://mendocino.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=31651D19-CF97-40E1-9DDC-87F4DB4D329F&ID=8000664&Options=&Search=
