## Why this candidate matters

The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board's official Waste Discharge Requirements and Monitoring and Reporting Program establish the exact survey structure needed for a defensible settlement reference:

- LF-1 is an older unlined landfill unit within Foothill Sanitary Landfill.
- The top deck was partially closed with an evapotranspiration cover in 2006.
- The 2015 Order required the remaining LF-1 closure to be completed by 15 November 2018 and certified within 30 days.
- The Order requires a survey of the final cover after closure for later comparison.
- The Monitoring and Reporting Program requires five-year iso-settlement surveys for closed units or portions such as LF-1.
- It requires a map accurately depicting estimated total elevation change of the final cover's low-hydraulic-conductivity layer or evapotranspiration cover.
- The next survey was due 15 November 2017, with the topographic survey and iso-settlement maps due 15 December 2017 and every five years thereafter. A subsequent submission was therefore due in 2022.

This is stronger than visual inspection language because the official program requires actual quantitative matched topographic products.

## Public retrieval outcome

Searches of the Water Board order pages, the 2025 amendment record, CIWQS public pages, San Joaquin County pages, and indexed web records did not retrieve the actual 2017 or 2022 topographic/iso-settlement maps.

The 2025 amendment concerns a proposed Class II surface impoundment and continuing leachate/landfill-gas-condensate management. The County's public comments state that LF-1 still generates condensate and that substantial operational changes were underway. The amendment does not expose or summarize the missing 2017/2022 elevation maps.

The public screenshot service returned cache-miss errors for the relevant official PDFs. Parsed official text was available, but no unrendered map, drawing, or figure was interpreted visually.

## Why it is not the ideal clean one-event cap

LF-1 is not a simple isolated post-2015 closure polygon:

- its top deck was partially closed in 2006;
- the 2015 Order required completion of remaining side-slope closure by 2018;
- active LF-2 modules encircle and partly overlap LF-1;
- landfill-gas, condensate, drainage, and other infrastructure remain active;
- the broader Foothill facility remained operational through 2025 and entered a separate phased long-term operational closure beginning in 2026; and
- the public record does not yet prove a construction-free, infrastructure-free interior polygon tied to a single 2018 as-built event.
