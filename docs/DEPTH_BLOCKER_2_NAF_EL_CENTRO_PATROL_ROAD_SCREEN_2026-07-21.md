Status: `strong_candidate_under_review`; not calibration-ready.

## Candidate

- Site: Naval Air Facility El Centro IRP Site 2, Patrol Road Landfill, California.
- Approximate site area: 39 acres.
- Unit 2 was a municipal landfill from about 1946 to 1965.
- The selected remedy retained landfill contents beneath an asphalt cap at Unit 2.
- The remedial action was completed in 2018.
- The Navy continues inspections and maintenance of the asphalt cover, drainage, monitoring wells, and settlement monuments.

## Construction completion evidence

The Colorado River Basin Regional Water Quality Control Board reports that it reviewed the October 2019 `Final Remedial Action Completion Report, Patrol Road Landfill, Naval Air Facility El Centro Installation Restoration Site 2`.

The regulator states that the report documents completion of the ROD and remedial-action work-plan requirements. A concurrence page signed by the Navy, DTSC, and the Regional Water Board certifies that remedial-action objectives were achieved. Regional Board concurrence was issued in August 2020.

The actual RACR, as-built drawings, survey surface, cap section, datum, and construction-accuracy statement were not retrieved from the currently accessible public index.

## Critical limitation — later repair work

The Regional Water Board's 2023 and 2024 executive-officer reports document review and concurrence for a `Landfill Cap Repair and Drainage Ditch and Culvert Repair` work plan at IRP Site 2.

Therefore the interval after the 2018 cap is not automatically a clean settlement-only interval. The repair footprint, dates, materials, regrading, and effects on settlement monuments must be known and masked before any later survey can be compared with the 2018 as-built surface.

## Missing matched survey evidence

The public Navy summary confirms settlement monuments and continued monitoring but does not publish monument coordinates, baseline elevations, repeat elevations, datum, accuracy, or a full-surface repeat survey.

The public Administrative Record portal is linked by NAVFAC but returned a gateway error during this screen. GeoTracker identifies the case as `DOD100317400`, but the actual report attachments were not retrieved from the indexed public pages used here.

## Decision

Retain as a strong retrieval target, but do not use it for calibration. The 2018 completion package and settlement-monument network are promising; however, no actual matched elevation series or surface pair has been retrieved, and later cap/drainage repairs contaminate the post-closure interval unless a clean polygon and unaffected monuments can be proven.
