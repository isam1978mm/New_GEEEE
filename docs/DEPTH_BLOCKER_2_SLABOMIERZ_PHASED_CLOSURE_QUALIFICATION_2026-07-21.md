# Depth Blocker 2 — Słabomierz–Krzyżówka Phased Closure Qualification — 2026-07-21

Status: `strong_settlement_reference_only`; `one_event_final_cap = fail`.

No email, form, operator contact, author contact, or records request was sent.

## New official evidence

The official PGK Żyrardów procurement archive and May 2016 replacement construction design prove that closure/reclamation was phased over many years rather than completed as one clean post-2015 cap event.

- Earlier perimeter cutoff, leachate drainage, retention and access works were completed and accepted for use in 2012.
- The 2016 design states that landfill-body shaping was still in progress using permitted processed mineral waste and that the remaining work included final shaping, drainage, roads, mineral cover and biological reclamation.
- The design explicitly describes phased execution.
- Stage I closure/reclamation works were tendered in April 2017 and an award notice was published on 14 June 2017.
- Stage II in 2018 was an access-road project rather than a single final-cap construction event.
- A further closure/reclamation procurement was published in 2021.
- Stage III financing was signed on 27 December 2023 for 3.5 ha of crown earthworks, mineral clay sealing, soil-forming cover and seeding.

The 2016 design also states that the existing crown was near 173 m elevation and the designed crown near 175 m, with approximately 2 m of added cover/reclamation material. This remains design evidence, not a certified as-built surface.

## Consequence

The March 2023 and March 2024 UAV point clouds remain unusually strong matched settlement-monitoring evidence, with independent centimetre-level checks. However, the site fails the ideal `one_event_final_cap_or_closure` criterion because closure, shaping and reclamation were distributed across multiple stages and years.

The public record does not yet prove that any specific survey polygon was untouched by Stage III or other construction between the two UAV epochs. Crown settlement, repair-soil placement, ponding, erosion, vegetation and slope movement also require masks.
