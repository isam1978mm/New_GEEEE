# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 Benton Crossing Candidate — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 1 future-watch only. Benton Crossing Landfill, Mono County, California, is not a completed calibration candidate. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- Mono County states that Benton Crossing Landfill stopped accepting waste and closed to the public on 31 December 2022.
- The 2020 closure plan, updated in 2021, describes a final-cover system, closure CQA, final aerial topographic survey, permanent control points, six settlement monuments, and five-year iso-settlement mapping.
- The same plan states that the first settlement-monument survey and aerial survey occur only after final closure construction is complete.
- The Lahontan Regional Water Board did not adopt dedicated closure/post-closure waste-discharge requirements until 2025.
- Mono County was still bidding an intermediate-cover project in June 2025.
- No final CQA report, closure-completion certification, final as-built contour package, or later repeat iso-settlement survey was publicly located.

## Decision

Do not promote Benton Crossing. The public record proves landfill operations ended in the Sentinel-1 period and that the approved closure design has an unusually strong survey framework, but it does not prove final closure construction was completed.

The 2025 intermediate-cover bid and 2025 closure/post-closure order show that the site was still progressing toward final closure. Therefore there is no valid final as-built baseline and no later repeat settlement survey yet.

## Next step

Continue screening another landfill whose final cap was completed by 2020–2022 so that a later repeat survey could already exist. Keep Benton Crossing on a future-watch list for its eventual closure survey and five-year iso-settlement map.
