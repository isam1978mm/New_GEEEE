# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 Bonzi Sanitation Landfill Candidate — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 1 active. Bonzi Sanitation Landfill, Stanislaus County, California, is retained as `candidate_under_review`. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified official public facts

- Central Valley Water Board Order R5-2024-0042 states that final-cover construction was substantially completed on 4 February 2022 and all closure work was completed on 28 April 2022.
- WMUs II, III, and IV were closed in 2022. The order describes approximately 58 acres of new final closure cover.
- The constructed cover system consists of a 2-foot compacted foundation layer, a 40-mil LLDPE low-permeability layer, geocomposite drainage on slopes steeper than 10 percent, and a minimum 1.5-foot vegetative/protective soil layer.
- The Water Board approved the final construction quality assurance report for the 2022 final closure cover on 21 June 2022.
- California requirements incorporated into the order require an initial final-cover survey and map after closure, followed every five years by a survey and iso-settlement map showing estimated elevation change across the final cover.
- Because closure occurred in 2022, the first required five-year repeat survey is expected in 2027 unless an earlier voluntary survey exists.
- No public copy of the approved 2022 CQA report, native as-built points/contours, stated survey accuracy, or later repeat topographic survey was located in this screen.
- Screenshot attempts on the official 117-page PDF failed with a cache-miss error; conclusions above come from the PDF's parsed official text, not visual extraction of drawings.

## Decision

Retain Bonzi as a high-priority candidate because it has a large completed 2022 closure, an approved final CQA report, a regulator-required initial final-cover survey, and a defined future repeat-survey requirement.

Do not promote it to calibration-ready. The approved CQA/as-built package is not publicly extracted, the pre-cover/top-of-waste surface is unresolved, survey datum and accuracy are unresolved, and the first mandatory repeat iso-settlement survey is not due until 2027.
