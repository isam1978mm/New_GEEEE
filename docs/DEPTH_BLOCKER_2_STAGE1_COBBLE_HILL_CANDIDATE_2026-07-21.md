Status: Stage 1 active. Cobble Hill Holdings Landfill, British Columbia, is retained as `candidate_under_review`. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- The Province of British Columbia identifies the site as a contaminated-soil landfill that entered permanent closure under Spill Prevention Order MO1701.
- The updated final closure plan was approved on June 26, 2019.
- Final closure construction was completed in fall 2020.
- The regulator publishes a September 2020, 71 MB construction wrap-up report and later post-closure monitoring reports.
- The public record also identifies construction-quality management, detailed construction planning, qualified-professional oversight, and as-built survey records.
- Search-indexed plan text states that as-built survey records were expected to confirm infrastructure inverts and grades.
- The accessible indexed material did not expose certified final cap surface elevations, the numerical accuracy of the final cap survey, or a survey-grade settlement table comparing construction completion with a later observation date.
