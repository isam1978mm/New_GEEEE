Status: Stage 1 active. Dane County Landfill Site No. 2 (Rodefeld), Wisconsin, Stage 4 final cover is retained as `candidate_under_review`. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- Dane County publicly bid a combined project titled `Phase 9, Cell 2 Liner and Stage 4 Final Cover Construction` in January 2018.
- The same official bid page lists a project manual, construction plan set, multiple project-data ZIP archives, and an `Addendum No. 1 - Final Cover Surfaces.zip` file.
- A separate official CQA procurement states that the Stage 4 final-cover project and Phase 9 Cell 2 liner project were planned as concurrent spring-to-fall 2018 construction projects.
- Dane County's habitat-restoration page states that a 2018–2019 final-cover project planted pollinator-friendly vegetation across about 35 acres of closed landfill space.
- A related official 2018 restoration procurement describes approximately 40 acres of capped landfill.
- These records establish a Sentinel-1-era, hectare-scale final-cover project with formal CQA oversight and publicly listed design-surface files.
- The public records located so far do not expose the final approved CQA report, certified as-built surface, native survey points, survey datum/accuracy, a clean immediate pre-cap surface, or a later repeat topographic/settlement survey.
- The final-cover work occurred concurrently with construction of a new landfill cell liner. The projects appear spatially distinct, but the Stage 4 cap footprint must be isolated before treating the event as a clean radar experiment.
