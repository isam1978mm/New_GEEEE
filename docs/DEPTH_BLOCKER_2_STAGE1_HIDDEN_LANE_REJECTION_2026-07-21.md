# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 Hidden Lane Landfill Rejection — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 1 remains active. Hidden Lane Landfill is rejected for the direct known-depth positive lane. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, model fitting, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- EPA identifies Hidden Lane as a former approximately 25-acre landfill in Sterling, Virginia.
- EPA selected the source-area remedy in February 2022.
- The remedy combines landfill-cap repair and maintenance with excavation and off-site disposal of principal-threat material.
- EPA reports that source-area excavation work completed in November 2024 removed about 15,000 tons of contaminated soil and debris.
- The excavated area was backfilled with clean fill and topsoil, graded, and hydroseeded.
- EPA's schedule shows additional source-area remedial work continuing after that excavation phase, and the overall site has not reached construction-complete status.

## Decision

Do not use Hidden Lane as a direct depth-positive candidate. The modern event is a mixed excavation, removal, backfill, grading, and cap-repair project rather than a clean whole-cell closure-in-place event over a verified buried interface.

The site may remain useful only for method research on strong surface-construction confounders.
