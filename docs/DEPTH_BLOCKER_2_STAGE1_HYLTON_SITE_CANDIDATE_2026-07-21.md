# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 Hylton Site Candidate — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 1 active. Hylton Site Sanitary Landfill, Bedford, Virginia, is retained as `candidate_under_review`. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, model fitting, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- The Town of Bedford advertised a defined closure-capping project for approximately 9 acres at the Hylton Site Sanitary Landfill in 2020.
- The advertised work included grading to permit grades, geomembrane and geonet-composite installation, anchor trench construction, protective cover, topsoil, vegetation, slope drains, stormwater ditches, and permanent drainage structures.
- The Town's fiscal-year 2022 audited financial statements identify the closed landfill as SWP 569, state that it began accepting waste in 1994, and state that it became 100% full and closed during 2021.
- The Town's fiscal-year 2021–2022 budget reports approximately $2.0 million in landfill-closure contractor spending during fiscal year 2021, consistent with construction occurring in the Sentinel-1 era.
- Virginia closure rules require professional-engineer closure certification with CQA/QC results and a survey plat after closure.
- The publicly indexed Town and state material did not expose the actual final CQA report, record drawings, surveyed final contours, numerical survey accuracy, pre-cap or top-of-waste survey, or a later measured topographic/settlement survey.

## Decision

Retain Hylton because it is a recent, defined landfill-cap project with a clear closure year and a discrete footprint. Unlike many rejected sites, the public project description does not indicate waste excavation, off-site relocation, redevelopment, stream restoration, or construction of buildings over the cap.

Do not promote it. The current record proves a closure project and closure timing, but it does not yet provide the measured surfaces required by the calibration contract.
