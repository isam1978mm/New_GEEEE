# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 Lower Darby Creek Rejection — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 1 remains active. Lower Darby Creek Area, Clearview Landfill, is rejected for the clean direct depth-calibration lane. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- The site contains the Clearview and Folcroft landfills.
- EPA began the Clearview remedial action in 2017 and landfill construction in 2019.
- The remedy constructed about 54 acres of forested evapotranspiration cover.
- Contaminated residential and park soils were excavated, moved, and in part placed under the cover.
- The work also included PCB removal, streambank stabilization, drainage work, irrigation, dense planting, grading, and later landfill-gas construction.
- EPA reports the main landfill construction completed in May 2025, with additional gas-control work continuing.

## Decision

Do not use Lower Darby Creek as a clean direct depth-positive candidate. Its radar change would combine cap placement with major excavation, imported soil, grading, wetland and streambank work, irrigation, and rapid vegetation establishment.

Retain only as method research for confounder testing.

## Next step

Keep North Sanitary and Watson Johnson as the two strongest active modern candidates and search for a third site with a smaller, cleaner as-built package.
