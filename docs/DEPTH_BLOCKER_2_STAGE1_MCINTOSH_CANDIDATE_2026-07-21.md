# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 McIntosh Candidate — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 2 records search completed. Lakeland Electric's C.D. McIntosh Power Plant Byproduct Storage Area, Florida, remains `candidate_under_review`. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- Lakeland Electric identifies the unit as an approximately 44-acre CCR landfill containing approximately 2.91 million cubic yards of CCR.
- The landfill was closed by leaving CCR in place and installing a final cover.
- The public closure notification is PE-certified and states final closure construction was completed in January 2025.
- The closure plan specifies construction quality assurance, construction certification, CAD-derived geometry, and a final cover system.
- The Florida DEP closure permit requires the engineer of record, after construction, to certify closure completion to the Department on Form 62-701.900(2), including detailed descriptions of any deviations from the approved closure plan.
- The permit incorporates the application, permit drawings, closure plan, and other design-stage documents, but the accessible permit package does not include the completed state construction-certification form or final as-built survey package.
- Lakeland Electric's public CCR page posts the federal one-page Notification of Closure Completion, but not the underlying state construction-certification report, as-built drawings, final cap elevations, survey-control notes, or stated survey accuracy.
- The December 10, 2025 annual inspection confirms the site was in post-closure care and describes a geomembrane liner, geocomposite drain material on slopes, two feet of cover material, and sod.
- The 2025 inspection reviewed aerial photography and inspection records, not a repeat survey-grade elevation dataset.
- Searches of indexed Florida DEP, OCULUS, Lakeland Electric, and public-file records did not retrieve the missing certification package or a later topographic survey.
- The closure notification contains an apparent date inconsistency: it says work was substantially completed in December 2025 but final construction was completed in January 2025. No correction was inferred.

## Decision

Retain McIntosh as an active candidate, but do not promote it.

The records search confirmed that a stronger state closure-certification submission should exist, but the publicly retrievable material does not expose the numerical survey evidence required by the calibration contract. The federal one-page completion notice proves closure occurred; it does not prove final cap elevations, measurement accuracy, or later settlement.

Do not use the two-foot cover description as depth ground truth. It describes cover construction, not a survey-derived buried-object depth label.

Stop spending search time on currently indexed McIntosh records. Keep it active for a future public-record request or newly posted file, and resume screening for another post-2015 whole-cap candidate whose construction and repeat-survey package is already directly accessible.
