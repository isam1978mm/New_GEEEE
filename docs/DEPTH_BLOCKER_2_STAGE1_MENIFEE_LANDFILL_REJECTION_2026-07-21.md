# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 Menifee Landfill Rejection — 2026-07-21

Status: Rejected for direct depth calibration. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized from this site.

## Verified official public facts

- Riverside County approved the Menifee Landfill Final Cover and Drainage Project contract documents in November 2018 and awarded the construction contract in January 2019.
- Riverside County recorded project completion in June 2019 and accepted the notice of completion in July 2019.
- The official county project description states that the work recovered exposed waste, reconsolidated offsite waste back within the property limits, constructed a drainage system, and installed an updated model-airplane runway and shade structure.
- County proceedings also show a separate 2018–2019 contract for imported earthen material at Menifee and other closed landfills.
- These activities mean the observed surface change cannot be treated as a clean final-cover-only elevation change over waste left undisturbed.
- No public final as-built survey pair, explicit survey accuracy, or later settlement/topographic survey tied to an isolated cap-only footprint was located during this screen.

## Decision

Reject Menifee for direct calibration. The 2019 surface change mixed cover placement with waste recovery and reconsolidation, imported soil, drainage construction, and surface redevelopment. A radar or elevation difference cannot be assigned specifically to cap thickness or settlement.
