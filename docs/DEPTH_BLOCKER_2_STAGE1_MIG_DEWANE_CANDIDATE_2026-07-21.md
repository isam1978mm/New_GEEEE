# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 MIG/DeWane Candidate — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 1 complete. MIG/DeWane Landfill, Illinois, is rejected for direct depth calibration as `rejected_not_clean_whole_cap`. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized from this candidate.

## Verified public facts

- EPA records final remedial action from October 2014 through March 2017 and construction completion on July 27, 2017.
- EPA completed a first five-year review in December 2019 and a second review on November 22, 2024.
- The 2013 remedy update explicitly allowed improvements to the existing landfill cover instead of installing a new cover system.
- The documented cover work added compacted clay mainly to side slopes, followed by grading, at least six inches of topsoil, seeding, and installation of landfill-gas wells and vents.
- Current operation and maintenance emphasizes inspections, quarterly groundwater measurements and sampling, landfill-gas monitoring, and leachate management.
- The publicly indexed records reviewed did not expose certified final cap-surface elevations, explicit horizontal or vertical survey accuracy, or a survey-grade settlement/topographic comparison between construction completion and a later observation.

## Decision

Reject MIG/DeWane for direct calibration.

The 2017 event is not a clean, newly installed whole-landfill cap. It is an improvement program applied to an existing cover, with work concentrated on side slopes and accompanied by grading, vegetation, gas-system construction, and continuing groundwater/leachate work. Those overlapping changes prevent the event from serving as a clean Sentinel-1 experiment unit even if later survey drawings are found.

The site may remain useful as a method-only example for detecting broad remedial disturbance, but it must not supply depth labels or calibration rows.

## Next step

Continue screening completed post-2015 whole-cap projects whose public closure reports contain as-built surfaces or contours, stated survey control or accuracy, and a later comparable topographic survey.

The next candidate to inspect is the Johns-Manville on-site landfill closure in Waukegan, Illinois, because EPA identifies a 2017 completion, 2018 closure inspections, a public final closure report, and a 2023 five-year review.
