# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 Pitchess Landfill Candidate — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 1 watch candidate. Peter J. Pitchess Detention Center Landfill, Los Angeles County, California, is not promoted to calibration use. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, model fitting, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- Los Angeles County’s closure project covers approximately 15 acres.
- The closure design calls for a minimum 3-foot final cover, drainage and erosion-control systems, a landfill-gas probe network, an access road, and vegetative cover.
- The closure project received a Notice of Determination approval on 5 January 2021.
- California CIWQS lists the Peter Pitchess Landfill Closure Project as a regulated facility associated with Los Angeles County Department of Public Works beginning 26 May 2021.
- California Title 27 requires a baseline as-closed topographic survey after closure and, where required by the Regional Water Board, a five-year iso-settlement map measured against that baseline.
- The public records located in this pass did not expose a final closure certification, completed CQA report, final as-built contour survey, numerical survey accuracy, or a later repeat iso-settlement/topographic survey.

## Decision

Do not treat the January 2021 approval as proof that closure construction was completed. Retain Pitchess only as a watch candidate until an official completion package is obtained.

The candidate would become materially stronger if the following are found:

- final closure certification and approval date;
- final CQA report;
- baseline as-closed survey or record drawings;
- survey-control network, horizontal/vertical datum, and numerical accuracy;
- confirmation that waste remained in place without major relocation;
- a later repeat topographic or iso-settlement survey using comparable control.
