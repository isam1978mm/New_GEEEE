# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 Rodemacher Fly Ash Pond Candidate — 2026-07-21

Status: Rejected for direct depth calibration. Cleco Power's Brame Energy Center / Rodemacher Unit 2 Fly Ash Pond, Louisiana, does not provide a clean cap-over-known-depth experiment. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified public facts

- Cleco's public CCR record lists a Fly Ash Pond closure certification dated December 11, 2023 and Louisiana closure approval dated June 3, 2024.
- The December 13, 2023 PE annual inspection identifies a maximum surface-impoundment area of 43.3 acres and states closure construction had recently been completed.
- The same inspection states that fly ash was removed from the pond and placed in landfill Cell 4.
- It also states that removal of fly-ash material was complete and that material was removed in support of final closure.
- The inspection was based on visible features and did not provide a repeat survey-grade elevation comparison, numerical horizontal or vertical survey accuracy, or settlement table.
- The closure-certification PDF repeatedly timed out during review, so no missing survey values were inferred from its title or from the later approval letter.

## Decision

Reject Rodemacher Fly Ash Pond for direct calibration.

The closure was not a cap placed over a stable, known waste surface. The ash was excavated from the pond and transferred to landfill Cell 4, changing the source area and creating a separate receiving-area disturbance. That prevents a controlled interpretation of radar change as cap thickness or depth-to-top.

The site may still be useful as a negative or method-only example showing why closure certification alone is insufficient when the remedy involves material relocation.

## Next step

Continue screening post-2015 single-unit closures that retained waste in place, published final as-built contours or survey tables, stated numerical survey accuracy, and later repeated the same surface survey.
