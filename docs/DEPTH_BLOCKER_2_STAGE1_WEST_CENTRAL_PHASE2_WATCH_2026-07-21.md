# Blocker 2 — Stage-1 West Central Phase 2 Watch — 2026-07-21

Status: Stage 1 watch only. West Central Landfill Phase 2 western partial closure, Shasta County, California, is not yet accepted as a calibration candidate. Blocker 2 remains unresolved. No calibration-pack intake, training, or app-depth enablement is authorized.

## Verified official public facts

- Waste Discharge Requirements Order R5-2015-0089 identified a proposed partial final closure of approximately 37 acres on the western portion of the Phase 2 Unit.
- The approved cover design consists of a two-foot soil foundation layer, geotextile cushion, 60-mil LLDPE geomembrane, and one-foot erosion-resistant vegetative soil layer.
- The 2015 order originally required completion by 1 November 2018 and required a baseline survey of the constructed cover for later five-year iso-settlement comparison.
- Order R5-2018-0054 states that the closure schedule was postponed: initiation moved to 2020 and final-cover completion was extended to 1 November 2020.
- The official regulatory framework requires an iso-settlement survey at closure completion and every five years afterward. If the 2020 closure was actually completed, the first repeat survey would normally fall around 2025.
- Public searching did not locate a closure-completion certification, final CQA/as-built report, baseline surface, native survey points, or a 2025 repeat iso-settlement map for this specific 37-acre footprint.
- The available crawler parsed the official PDFs, but screenshot rendering of relevant pages failed with cache errors. No unrendered drawing or figure was visually interpreted.

## Decision

Keep West Central Phase 2 as a records-request watch only. The planned area and cover system are large enough to matter, and a 2020-to-2025 survey pair could be valuable. However, the public evidence currently proves only the revised schedule and survey requirements—not actual completion or the existence of a usable repeat elevation dataset.

Do not promote it until the closure-completion certification and matched survey surfaces are obtained.
