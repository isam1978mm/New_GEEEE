## Candidate 1 — Standard Chlorine of Delaware, Inc.

### Official-source facts

- EPA selected a multi-layer geotextile and soil cap for the former plant area.
- Construction began in May 2015 and cap construction was completed in spring 2017.
- EPA amended the remedy in February 2016 so that soils from the OU-2 waste pile would be placed within the OU-3 multilayer cap.

### Decision

Reject as a clean cap-thickness or settlement calibration reference. The event includes deliberate relocation and placement of waste-pile soils beneath the new cap, so observed elevation change cannot be attributed to final-cover construction alone.

## Candidate 2 — Velsicol Chemical Corp. (Hardeman County), Tennessee

### Official-source facts

- EPA modified the remedy in 2012 to include landfill-cap extension and drainage improvements across the waste-disposal-area landfills.
- Cap construction and drainage improvements were completed in May 2016.
- Construction of a soil-vapor-extraction component began in March 2017.

### Decision

Reject as a clean one-event calibration reference. The 2016 cap-extension event is entangled with drainage work and subsequent SVE construction beginning in 2017, preventing a defensible attribution of later elevation or radar change to cap settlement alone.

Official reference:

- EPA Superfund site profile, Velsicol Chemical Corp. (Hardeman County): `https://cumulis.epa.gov/supercpad/SiteProfiles/index.cfm?fuseaction=second.cleanup&id=0403886`
