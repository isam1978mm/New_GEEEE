# F174 — Bremo West Ash Pond: later survey, volume reference, and lidar screen

Date: 2026-08-21

## Purpose

Continue the elevation-only re-score of Bremo West Ash Pond after direct inspection of the project-source county packet in F173.

## New official/public evidence

1. A later Dominion/Golder West Ash Pond closure plan (2018/2019) states that existing topography inside West Ash Pond is based on an H&B Surveying and Mapping field survey dated 2017-07-06 and that the topography represents the pond bottom.
2. The same closure plan shows a 50 ft x 50 ft PRE- and POST-excavation survey grid and requires Visual Clean followed by at least six inches of over-excavation.
3. The March 2020 CQA report gives the construction chronology:
   - CBR construction began approximately 2016-07-06.
   - CCR excavation occurred from July 2016 through July 2017, then paused.
   - CBR work resumed approximately 2019-09-28.
   - Final substantial completion was 2020-01-30.
4. The CQA report records West Ash Pond as approximately 17.0 acres and reports:
   - Initial/VC removal: 327,323 cubic yards.
   - Six-inch removal: 21,690 cubic yards.
   - Total: 349,013 cubic yards.
5. The Dominion closure plan independently states that the pond contained approximately 327,000 cubic yards of CCR before excavation.

## Volume-derived mean thickness

Using the official CQA values only as a volume/area normalization:

- 327,323 yd3 over 17.0 acres corresponds to an average thickness of approximately 11.93 ft = 3.64 m.

This is **NOT labeled a surveyed point/zone depth**. It is only a **volume-derived pond-wide average reference** because the public record does not explicitly establish how the 327,323 yd3 quantity itself was computed (survey-to-survey earthwork model, load records, or another quantity method).

Therefore it is useful as an independent scalar cross-check, but it is not yet a validated spatial depth anchor.

## Lidar replacement screen

A promising 2014 USGS Central Virginia Seismic lidar dataset was screened as a possible pre-excavation substitute.

Dataset facts:
- acquisition: 2014-05-06 to 2014-05-07;
- nominal spacing <= 0.7 m;
- tested open-terrain RMSEz approximately 0.064 m;
- geographic bounds: west -78.15526, east -77.707851, north 38.140154, south 37.766376.

Bremo Power Station is approximately 37.7053 N, -78.2894 W.

Decision: **FAIL — no Bremo coverage.** The dataset stops both north and east of the plant.

Do not use or interpolate this lidar to Bremo.

## Current Bremo status

Bremo remains **HIGH-PRIORITY OPEN**, but the directly executable full-depth surface pair is still incomplete.

Known useful surfaces/records:

- Official volume-derived scalar reference: approximately 3.64 m pond-wide average CCR thickness from 327,323 yd3 / 17.0 acres, but measurement provenance is not explicit enough to call it a measured depth anchor.
