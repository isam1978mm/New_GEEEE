# F177 — Bremo West Ash Pond volume-provenance test

Date: 2026-08-21

## Purpose

Test whether the official Bremo West Ash Pond excavation quantity can be upgraded from a volume-derived reference into independent measured depth truth without recovering the missing June 9, 2016 H&B pre-excavation surface.

The proposed rescue was:

> if the official 327,323 yd3 Visual-Clean removal quantity is explicitly proven to be survey-to-survey earthwork volume, then dividing that measured volume by a measured excavation area could provide a defensible zone-mean excavation depth.

## Official CQA evidence

The March 2020 West Ash Pond Construction Quality Assurance report states:

- West Ash Pond is approximately 17.0 acres.
- CCR excavation began in July 2016, paused after July 2017, and resumed in September 2019.
- After the contractor established a Visually Clean (VC) surface, a topographic survey was performed to establish the horizontal and vertical limits of the VC surface.
- At least six inches of additional over-excavation was then performed.
- A final survey of the over-excavated area was performed to verify at least six inches had been removed, except where competent material prevented further excavation.
- Surveying was provided by Flora Surveying Associates under contract to Ryan Incorporated Central.
- The report lists approximate excavation volumes:
  - VC Removal: 327,323 yd3
  - Six-Inch Removal: 21,690 yd3
  - Total: 349,013 yd3

## Provenance decision

The accessible official report text calls these **approximate volumes** but does **not explicitly state** that the 327,323 yd3 VC-removal quantity was calculated by differencing a measured pre-excavation survey surface against the measured VC surface.

The report separately proves that licensed topographic surveys exist for the VC and final six-inch-over-excavated surfaces, but this does not by itself prove the provenance of the earlier 327,323 yd3 quantity.

Therefore the quantity cannot yet be upgraded to a measured depth anchor.

## Derived value status

327,323 yd3 normalized over approximately 17 acres gives about:

- 11.93 ft
- 3.64 m

This remains labeled:

> **volume-derived pond-wide average reference**

It is **not** labeled:

- measured depth;
- surveyed mean depth;
- validated calibration anchor.

## Why the distinction matters

Using the 3.64 m value as measured truth without explicit quantity provenance would silently assume the missing fact that the excavation volume was survey-to-survey and that the 17-acre denominator exactly matches the volume footprint.

That would violate the project's independent-validation safeguards.

## Current Bremo status

Bremo remains scientifically strong but numerically access-blocked.

Known:
- June 9, 2016 H&B pre-excavation field survey existed, geometry not recovered.
- July 2017 H&B pond-bottom/post-excavation survey existed.
- 2019/2020 licensed VC and six-inch survey surfaces existed and Appendix A contains survey drawings.
- CQA excavation quantities exist.

Missing for a defensible full-depth validation:
- explicit survey-derived provenance for the 327,323 yd3 quantity, or
- the actual pre-excavation surface to difference directly.

## Next action

The next useful Bremo artifact is the actual March 2020 CQA PDF / Appendix A survey package in Project Sources. Inspect it directly for:

1. quantity-computation methodology;
2. survey notes/drawing metadata;
3. numerical or digitizable VC and six-inch surfaces;
4. any reference to the pre-excavation survey used for excavation quantities.

Do not upgrade 3.64 m to measured truth unless that direct source proves the missing provenance.
