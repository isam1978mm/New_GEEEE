# F182 — Bremo Virginia Base Mapping Program aerial timing screen

Date: 2026-08-21

## Purpose

After F181 found no public lidar pair bracketing the 2019-2020 Bremo Visual Clean -> six-inch excavation interval, test the next realistic historical-surface source: Virginia Base Mapping Program (VBMP) aerial acquisition / photogrammetry.

The frozen CBR-02 truth values were not used to choose imagery.

## Public program chronology

Public VBMP descriptions state that the 2017-2019 update was divided into three flight phases:

- 2017 — eastern third of Virginia;
- 2018 — middle third of Virginia;
- 2019 — western third of Virginia.

Fluvanna is in central Virginia. Public neighboring-locality metadata documents 2018 VBMP orthophotography for the central-Virginia area, consistent with the middle-third flight schedule.

The VBMP project included aerial data acquisition, ground control, aerial triangulation, an ortho-quality DEM, and digital orthophotography. Therefore it is a real photogrammetric mapping program, not merely a web basemap.

## Timing decision

The Bremo truth interval that must be bracketed is approximately late September 2019 through January 30, 2020.

The relevant VBMP central-Virginia acquisition is 2018, before that interval. The 2019 VBMP phase was the western third of the state, not the central Fluvanna flight area.

Later public Fluvanna-area mapping references use a 2022 VGIN/VBMP acquisition, well after the 2019-2020 excavation.

### F182 decision

**FAIL at timing gate.**

There is no identified VBMP acquisition pair over Bremo that brackets the Visual Clean -> six-inch excavation interval.

Because timing fails, do not spend effort trying to recover raw VBMP frames, camera models, or stereo pairs for this specific six-inch validation.

## Important distinction

The 2018 VBMP imagery/DEM may still be useful for historical site interpretation, but it cannot be subtracted from the 2020 survey and labeled as the six-inch excavation. Significant construction occurred between those dates.
