# Clothier Disposal - Active Reuse Follow-up

Date: 2026-07-25

## Decision

**Status:** closed as a numerical-depth calibration candidate.

Clothier initially looked promising because its one-foot clean-soil cover was completed in 1992, the site remained protective through the 2022 five-year review, and groundwater monitoring wells were abandoned in 2017. It fails the unchanged-surface and measured-depth gates because a motocross track is now active on part of the property and the public record does not expose a close-out survey that proves the track is outside every covered area or provides measured cover thickness with numerical survey uncertainty.

## What is confirmed

- Site: Clothier Disposal, Town of Granby, Oswego County, New York; EPA ID NYD000511576.
- Approximately 2,200 drums and visibly contaminated soil/debris were removed during initial actions.
- The selected remedy placed a one-foot clean-soil cover over residually contaminated areas, regraded and revegetated the site, and restricted activities that could disturb the cover.
- Installation of the soil cover and revegetation was completed in 1992.
- Construction completion is recorded in 1993, and the site was deleted from the National Priorities List in 1996.
- Groundwater monitoring was discontinued and the monitoring wells were abandoned in 2017.
- EPA five-year reviews through November 2022 found the remedy protective.
- EPA's current reuse record states that a motocross track is active on part of the property.

## Why it is not usable

1. The one-foot value is a remedy design requirement, not an extracted as-built measured thickness for individual mapped cells.
2. The 1993 close-out report and certified as-built survey could not be retrieved from the public online collection.
3. The public record does not provide polygon vertices, coordinate reference system, final elevations, or numerical survey uncertainty.
4. An active motocross track means the site cannot be assumed to have an unchanged vegetated surface.
5. No public map proves that the track and all associated grading are completely outside the covered residual-contamination areas.
6. The reference is diffuse residual soil contamination rather than a discrete target.
