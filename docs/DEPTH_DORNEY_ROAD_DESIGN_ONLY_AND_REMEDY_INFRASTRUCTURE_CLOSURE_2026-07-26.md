# Dorney Road Landfill — Design-Only Cap and Remedy Infrastructure

Date: 2026-07-26

## Decision

**Status:** closed as unsuitable for numerical depth calibration.

Dorney Road Landfill has a completed and protected multi-layer cap, but the public record reviewed does not provide point-specific accepted as-built cover depths, surveyed measurement locations, or numerical uncertainty. The site also contains passive gas venting, permanent drainage works, monitoring infrastructure, quarterly inspections and restored wetlands.

## What is confirmed

- Site: Dorney Road Landfill, Pennsylvania.
- EPA ID: `PAD980508832`.
- The site is an approximately 27-acre former municipal and industrial landfill.
- EPA selected the landfill remedy in 1988.
- Remedy construction included:
  - reshaping surface contours;
  - a multi-layer engineered cap;
  - passive landfill-gas collection/venting;
  - dikes, diversion ditches and permanent stormwater controls;
  - fencing and institutional controls;
  - groundwater monitoring;
  - replacement wetlands.
- Construction-complete status was reached in September 1999.
- Replacement wetlands were constructed from 1999 through 2005.
- The site was deleted from the National Priorities List in September 2018.
- Groundwater monitoring and cap inspections continue quarterly.
- EPA's 2023 Five-Year Review found the remedy protective.
- Institutional controls prevent disturbance of the cap and restrict incompatible future use.
- The restored wetland system now includes established native habitat and open water.

## Why it is not usable

1. No public point-specific table of accepted final cover thicknesses was recovered.
2. No surveyed coordinates tied to actual local depth measurements were recovered.
3. No numerical construction tolerance, survey accuracy, measurement precision or finite uncertainty interval was recovered.
4. Remedy design and construction-complete statements cannot substitute for actual measured depth records.
5. Passive gas vents, drainage works, monitoring points, access areas and recurring O&M create spatial confounding.
6. Wetlands and open-water habitat create a dynamic surface that cannot be treated as uniform soil cover.
7. A protective and legally preserved cap does not by itself prove a stable, point-calibrated radar reference.
