# Depth validation — F167 Meredosia post-work surface gate — 2026-08-20

## Decision

**F167 BLOCKED for direct numerical excavation depth with the currently recovered public surfaces.**

Meredosia passed the pre-work lidar gate at F166, but the straightforward subtraction proposed initially is not scientifically valid because the excavated Bottom Ash Pond was subsequently backfilled to final grade.

This does not invalidate the broader elevation-only re-score strategy. It means Meredosia requires an intermediate construction surface or a spatially resolved backfill model before it can yield true excavation depth.

## Decisive official evidence

The 2024 Meredosia Bottom Ash Pond closure certification states that:

- CCR was excavated from the Bottom Ash Pond and hauled to the Fly Ash Pond;
- the CCR berm was not excavated and was capped;
- CCR removal occurred between March and May 2018;
- after CCR removal and CQA Officer approval, the areas were **brought to final grade using non-CCR backfill**;
- stormwater controls were then installed and the areas were vegetated;
- no post-removal subgrade sampling or analysis was conducted.

A separate conservation-plan record also states that clean material would be used to fill the Bottom Ash Pond.

Therefore:

> `December-2017 pre-work lidar - 2018/2019 final-grade surface` is not equal to true excavation depth.

The final surface contains replacement fill, so a simple before/after difference would underestimate the amount of CCR removed and could approach zero in areas restored toward the former grade.

## Post-work records known to exist

The following records are confirmed:

1. January 18, 2019 Geotechnology **Construction Quality Assurance Report — Closure of Bottom Ash Pond and Fly Ash Pond**;
2. Geotechnology / CDG Engineers **Ash Pond Closure, As-Built Plans, Meredosia Power Station (2019)**;
3. David Mason + Associates project survey products:
   - horizontal/vertical project control;
   - construction UAS surveys of the Bottom Ash Pond and Fly Ash Pond;
   - point clouds and DTMs;
   - post-construction UAS point cloud and DTM.

Ameren's current Meredosia page publicly lists a Construction Quality Assurance PDF, but the direct file is not retrievable through the current web runtime (403). Publicly indexed official material confirms the report and as-built plans exist but has not exposed a metric post-removal/pre-backfill terrain surface.

## What is still required to reopen Meredosia for numerical cut depth

Any one of the following would be sufficient to continue:

1. an **intermediate post-removal / pre-backfill survey or DTM** of the clean-closed Bottom Ash Pond;
2. as-built drawings containing the excavated-subgrade surface / spot elevations / contours before clean fill;
3. spatially resolved clean-backfill thickness or cut/fill model sufficient to reconstruct the excavation floor independently;
4. a construction UAS epoch demonstrably captured after CCR removal and before backfill.

A final post-construction DTM alone is not enough.

## Important mask guard

The Bottom Ash Pond berm was not clean-excavated and must remain excluded. Roadway/pipeline/infrastructure or other cap-in-place areas must also remain excluded unless separately modeled.
