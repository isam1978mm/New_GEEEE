# Depth validation — F169 John Sevier elevation re-score — 2026-08-20

## Decision

**F169 HIGH-PRIORITY OPEN — not yet executable for numerical depth.**

John Sevier is stronger under elevation-only criteria than it was under radar/Sentinel-1 screening because both a trustworthy pre-work terrain source and an as-built subgrade survey are documented.

## Pre-work geometry

TVA's public iSite GIS exposes FY2015 large-scale topographic contours created from aerial photography and LiDAR.

For John Sevier, the published Photo/LiDAR date is approximately **2014-11-04 through 2014-11-10**, well before closure excavation began in 2016.

The GIS includes **1 ft contours** and 5 ft index contours.

This establishes a real site-specific pre-work topographic source.

## Construction chronology

TVA construction-history records report:

- pool lowering: approximately Nov 2015 through Mar 2016;
- CCR stacking/excavation on the east side began approximately Apr 2016;
- final closure construction continued through 2016-2017.

Therefore the Nov-2014 TVA LiDAR/topographic surface is safely pre-excavation.

## Post-work / subgrade evidence

TVA's closure plan states that the maximum CCR inventory estimate was based on a comparison involving a **subgrade surface established by the as-built survey of the unit**.

This is important evidence that a numerical closure-stage subgrade survey existed.

## Confound that prevents simple final-surface subtraction

The closure plan also states that:

- CCR and some underlying soil were excavated from one portion;
- material was consolidated elsewhere;
- the western dike was regraded;
- excavated/dike soil was used for berm, cover and **fill material for grading**.

Therefore a simple comparison between the 2014 pre-work surface and a final 2017 closed surface can mix:

- CCR removal;
- underlying-soil excavation;
- dike regrading;
- replacement fill / grading;
- final cover.

That would not equal pure CCR thickness or pure excavation depth.

## What would make John Sevier executable

Recover one of:

1. the numerical **as-built subgrade survey surface** referenced by TVA;
2. a closure-stage grading/cut-fill model that separates excavated subgrade from replacement fill;
3. survey points/contours/breaklines for the subgrade condition before final fill/cover.

The current public indexed material proves the survey existed but has not exposed the numerical surface itself.
