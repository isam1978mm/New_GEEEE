# Garrett LLC / Former Dalton Foundry — Required Measurements but Redeveloped

Date: 2026-07-26

## Decision

**Status:** closed as unsuitable for depth calibration.

The official 2022 IDEM order required a measured post-construction closure package, but no public completion package, as-built grading plan, point-specific thickness table, or Resolution of Case letter was recovered. More importantly, the former foundry property has since undergone major building renovation for use as a school. That active redevelopment prevents the site from serving as a clean, unchanged Sentinel-1 calibration surface.

## What the official order required

- Case: IDEM `2020-27103-S`.
- The order was signed on October 11, 2022.
- The owner had one year to implement closure work.
- At least 18 inches of clay-type soil was required over exposed foundry waste; two feet was recommended.
- The final covered area had to be graded and vegetated.
- Within 30 days after completion, the owner had to submit:
  - an as-built grading plan depicting waste boundaries;
  - verification of soil-cover thickness using probes, hand augers, or test pits;
  - at least two borings or holes per acre.
- The order also required an O&M plan, twice-yearly inspections, maintenance of vegetative cover, and institutional restrictions.
- The order remained effective until IDEM issued a Resolution of Case letter.

## Why the order is not calibration evidence

The order describes work that **must be done**. It does not prove that:

- closure construction was completed;
- the required measurements were taken;
- IDEM accepted the work;
- the as-built plan or measured values exist in a retrievable public record;
- any specific point has a known final depth and numerical uncertainty.

No publicly indexed Resolution of Case letter or completed closure package was found.

## Later redevelopment

The same former-foundry property is now identified by the Islamic Academy of Kendallville as its school-building project. The academy reports substantial renovation of the building and active preparations/use for school operations during the 2025–2026 and 2026–2027 school years.

This creates decisive surface-change and land-use confounding:

- building renovation and construction activity;
- active institutional occupation;
- likely vehicle, drainage, landscaping, and utility changes;
- no proof that any cover-measurement point remained unchanged through the Sentinel-1 observation interval.
