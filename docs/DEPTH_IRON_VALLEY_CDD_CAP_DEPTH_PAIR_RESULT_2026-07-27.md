# Iron Valley C&DD Landfill cap depth-pair result - 2026-07-27

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

Iron Valley is a completed, full-scale vegetated closure with a professional construction certification, test-pit checks, and a final ground-grade survey. It satisfies several important evidence gates but does not provide two different measured depth conditions.

## Recovered construction evidence

The 2016 certification covers approximately 5.58 acres. The final cover specification is:

```text
minimum 18 inches compacted cohesive soil
minimum 6 inches vegetative soil
complete and dense vegetation
minimum total = 24 inches
```

Ten test pits were excavated to verify that the minimum compacted and vegetative layers existed. The area near TP-08 initially failed the minimum requirement, was reworked, and was checked again.

A September 2016 ground survey documented final grades. The final closure drawing also shows an older approximately 6.42-acre standard cap certified in 2001.

## Fatal problem

The older and newer vegetated cap areas are both standard caps. The package does not establish a shallow-versus-deep thickness difference between them.

The test pits were used as pass/fail minimum-thickness checks. The report does not publish actual final compacted-layer and vegetative-layer thickness values for each test pit.

The only materially different areas are gravel/asphalt roads and storage pads. Those have different radar-facing surfaces and cannot be used as the comparison condition.

clean area likely wider than 30-40 m = yes in portions

## Next step

Continue only with full-scale vegetated projects where separate certified closure phases have different final measured cap thicknesses, not merely the same minimum specification repeated across phases.
