# Iron Valley C&DD Landfill final-closure result - 2026-07-27

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

Iron Valley is a full-scale vegetated closure, but it does not provide the measured shallow/deep contrast required by the approved numerical-depth plan.

## Evidence recovered

The October 2016 cap-construction and final-closure certification documents the remaining 5.58 acres of cap construction.

The standard final cover consists of:

- minimum 18 inches of compacted cohesive soil;
- minimum 6 inches of soil capable of supporting dense vegetation;
- seeding and mulch to establish vegetative cover;
- minimum total vegetated cover thickness of 24 inches / 2.0 ft / 0.6096 m.

Ten test pits were used to verify that the minimum compacted-soil and vegetative-soil layers existed. The area near one test pit initially failed and was reworked and rechecked.

A professional ground survey was also completed on September 15 and 16, 2016 to document final grades.

## Fatal problem

The test pits verify compliance with one minimum cover specification. They do not establish two broad, final measured thickness zones that remain at least 30-40 m wide after margins.

The only alternative-cap areas are gravel/asphalt access roads and pads. Those areas have a different radar-facing surface and therefore cannot serve as the shallow side of a same-surface comparison.

This cannot be fixed by drawing different polygons. The source document does not contain the required measured depth contrast.
