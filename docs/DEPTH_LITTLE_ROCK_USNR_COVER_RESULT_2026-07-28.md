# Little Rock/Tyrone USNR cover-depth screen result

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

The reports contain real as-built thickness measurements and later stability monitoring, but the plots do not provide two different measured depth zones.

## What the official records prove

- The test plot covers approximately **2 acres**.
- It contains four treatments of approximately **0.5 acre each**.
- The treatments compare:
  - conventional versus experimental seed mix;
  - mulch applied before versus after seeding.
- The finished slopes are approximately 3:1 to 4:1, with surveyed slope lengths of approximately 150 to 180 ft.
- Cover thickness was checked in five test pits.
- All five test pits exceeded the common 36-inch minimum and measured **38 to 45 inches**.
- Monitoring through 2020 described the soil surfaces as stable and vegetation as generally successful.

## Fatal blocker

The treatments were not constructed at different depths. They use one common cover-depth requirement and differ in seed mix and mulch timing.

The 38-to-45-inch test-pit range is scattered as-built variation, not two official shallow and deep polygons. Creating depth zones from those five points would require unsupported interpolation.

Each approximately half-acre treatment is also too small and slope-dominated to preserve an independent 30-40 m clean radar interior after excluding plot edges, erosion transects, instruments, and slope transitions.

two large exact final measured depth zones = no
