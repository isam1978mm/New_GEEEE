# Middlesex Sampling Plant depth-pair screen result

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

The completed soil cleanup does not provide a publicly usable numerical-depth calibration pair under the locked rules.

## What the public official record proves

- The site covers approximately **9.6 acres**.
- Soil remediation was completed during the 2006-2008 period.
- Remaining contaminated buildings and below-grade structures were removed.
- A final-status radiological survey was performed before backfilling.
- The accessible project paper describes **22 onsite survey units and one offsite survey unit**.

## Fatal blocker

Those final-status surveys were designed to verify radiological cleanup criteria. The accessible public record does not publish:

- exact measured excavation depths for the survey units;
- two broad shallow and deep depth zones;
- coordinate-tied final depth polygons;
- numerical horizontal, vertical, or depth uncertainty;
- proof that two independent 30-40 m clean interiors share the same restored surface.

The USACE project page lists the final Post-Remedial Action Report, but both isolated recovery attempts returned HTTP 403 HTML responses instead of the PDF. This decision therefore does **not** claim that the inaccessible report was inspected. It records that the required public measured-depth evidence could not be recovered or established.

The property was transferred to the Borough of Middlesex in 2024 for planned public-works redevelopment, so later surface stability cannot be assumed.
