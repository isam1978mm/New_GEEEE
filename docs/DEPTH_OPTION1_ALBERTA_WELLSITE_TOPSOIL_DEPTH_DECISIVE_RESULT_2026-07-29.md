# Option 1 decisive result — Alberta abandoned-wellsite topsoil-depth experiment

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the primary record proves

The Strathmore, Hesketh and Rosedale abandoned-wellsite experiment used:

- four topsoil-replacement treatments: 0%, 50%, 100% and 150% of the required replacement depth;
- five organic-amendment sub-treatments;
- four replicates at each of three sites;
- measured 100% treatment depths of about 8–9 cm, with reported variation;
- field monitoring from 1997 through 2000, with later resampling reported for 2007.

## Fatal blocker

The physical plots are far too small for radar calibration:

- each topsoil-depth main plot is 8 m by 10 m;
- each amendment subplot is 8 m by 2 m;
- no 30–40 m clean interior can exist after any boundary exclusion.

Additional blockers include amendment confounding, no official execution polygons, and no proof that exact plots remained identifiable and unchanged during the Sentinel-1 period.
