# Option 1 decisive result — Battle River subsoil-depth experiment

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the public technical record proves

The Battle River Soil Reconstruction Project provides a genuine long-term depth experiment:

- the project was established near Forestburg/Halkirk, Alberta, and the experimental plots were constructed in 1980–1981;
- the subsoil-depth experiment used the same general soil-material system over sodic mine spoil;
- all treatments received a common topsoil layer;
- prescribed subsoil treatments ranged from no subsoil to more than 3 m of subsoil;
- the experiment used three replicated blocks;
- plots were monitored annually during the 1980s and were resampled in 2013;
- the 2014 long-term study reports treatment depths of 0, 25, 65, 135, 165 and 335 cm beneath 15 cm of topsoil.

The experiment therefore passes the scientific-design screen for a same-material depth contrast and has unusually long follow-up.

## Fatal blockers

The public record does not provide the ground-truth package required for Option 1:

- the listed treatment depths are experimental treatment labels, not a published certified as-built thickness surface or same-coordinate before/after survey;
- no numerical construction-thickness or survey uncertainty is attached to the treatment labels;
- no official coordinate-tied treatment polygons, surveyed corners, GIS/CAD geometry, or execution datum are published;
- the randomized replicated layout is divided into treatment and crop subplots and contains permanent markers and monitoring tubes;
- the available scaled layout does not establish a conservative 30–40 m clean interior carrying one uniform depth label after boundary and instrumentation exclusions;
- the last documented plot resampling occurred in 2013, before the Sentinel-1 operating period used by this program;
- no public plot-specific record proves the exact treatment interiors remained unchanged and repair-free after 2014.

Assigning a broad radar polygon an exact depth such as 1.35 m or 3.35 m would therefore overstate what the public construction evidence supports.

## Reopening condition

Reopen only if an official or operator-issued package supplies:

1. final measured treatment thicknesses or surfaces with numerical uncertainty;
2. coordinate-controlled treatment boundaries and datum;
3. conservative clean interiors after crop, marker, tube, road and boundary exclusions; and
4. mapped post-2014 land-use, repair and stability records for the exact plots.
