# Option 1 decisive result — Boreal Shield Technosol lysimeter trial

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the public record proves

The trial evaluated manufactured Technosols for gold-mine reclamation on the Canadian Boreal Shield:

- crushed mine rock and woody residuals were mixed at two different ratios;
- the manufactured soils were placed at 30 cm and 60 cm depths;
- the treatments were installed on mine-rock lysimeters;
- soil microclimate and pore-water chemistry were monitored from late 2012 through 2015.

## Fatal blockers

- The depth treatments are lysimeter treatments, not broad field-scale reclamation polygons.
- The manufactured-soil composition changes between the 60:40 and 20:80 mine-rock/woody-residual mixtures, creating a material confound.
- No 30–40 m clean interior can exist within a lysimeter treatment.
- No official coordinate-tied field polygons, certified plot-wide thickness surface, or numerical construction uncertainty are published.
- The short monitoring interval does not provide a long, stable field-scale Sentinel-1 comparison.

The field-size failure is decisive. No Earth Engine work is justified.
