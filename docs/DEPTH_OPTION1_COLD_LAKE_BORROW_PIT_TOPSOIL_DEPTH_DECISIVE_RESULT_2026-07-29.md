# Option 1 decisive result — Cold Lake borrow-pit topsoil-depth trial

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the primary public paper proves

The northeastern Alberta borrow-pit trial compared:

- 80% of the pre-disturbance topsoil depth, approximately 22 cm;
- 50% of the pre-disturbance topsoil depth, approximately 14 cm;
- 50% topsoil plus peat;
- 50% topsoil plus biochar;
- three randomized replicate blocks;
- common boreal revegetation monitoring from 2015 through 2019.

The unamended 22 cm versus 14 cm treatments form a same-site depth comparison in principle.

## Fatal blocker

Each treatment plot is only 20 m by 20 m and is separated by 2 m buffers. A conservative interior is therefore smaller than 20 m in both dimensions and cannot satisfy the required 30–40 m radar-support width.

Additional blockers include:

- no certified plot-wide as-built thickness surface or numerical construction uncertainty;
- no official coordinate-tied treatment polygons suitable for execution;
- treatment infrastructure and transplanted vegetation;
- only a five-year ecological monitoring period.

The size failure is decisive. No Earth Engine work is justified.
