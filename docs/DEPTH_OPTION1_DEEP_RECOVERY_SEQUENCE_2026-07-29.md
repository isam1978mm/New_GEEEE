# Option 1 Deep Recovery Sequence — 2026-07-29

## Decision

**No candidate is good to go yet.**

This sequence replaced repeated low-yield random batches with targeted recovery on the strongest public near-misses.

## Candidates closed in this sequence

### Detour Lake Mine

- 10-hectare operational-scale cover trial.
- 13 plots.
- Nominal designs include 0, 0.3, 0.7 and 1.0 m cover thicknesses.
- Construction occurred in 2019 and five-year monitoring exists.
- Rejected because thickness is confounded with slope, aspect, grading, peat content and revegetation.
- Exact coordinate-tied plot polygons, measured final thicknesses and numerical uncertainty are not publicly available.

### Syncrude 1990 Oil Sands Tailings Capping Study

- Four treatment plots using 30, 50 and 70 cm cap designs.
- Public summary reports good qualitative control of cap thickness.
- Rejected because public records do not establish large clean interiors, exact plot polygons, numerical as-built measurements or stable survival into the Sentinel-1 period.

### Morenci Tailings Experimental Reclamation Plots

- 30 and 60 cm cover-depth treatments existed.
- Depth-comparison plots were only 7.6 m by 15.2 m.
- Rejected at the radar-size gate.

### South African Unidentified Gold Mine

- Four lysimeter cover designs from 450 to 800 mm, monitored from 2017 to 2021.
- Strong field monitoring and construction protocols.
- Rejected because the mine and coordinate-tied lysimeter geometry are intentionally undisclosed.
- Designs also differ structurally, not only in thickness.

### Century Mine

- Three 75 m by 75 m cover-trial cells.
- Constructed in 2013 with ten years of monitoring.
- Rejected because the cells test different reduced-permeability materials and barrier systems rather than a clean depth-only pair.

### Mt Whaleback

- Two one-hectare plots at 2 m and 4 m nominal thickness.
- Rejected because no construction QA, replication or confirmed control is available, and the radar-facing trial surfaces are not clean matched vegetated zones.

### Whistle Mine

- Engineered cover plateaus approximately 24 m by 12 m.
- Rejected at the clean-width gate.

## Active near-misses

### Aurora Soil Capping Study

Strong one-hectare replicated treatment layout and matched materials. Blocked by the private 2013 construction characterization and missing public as-built measurements.

### Aitik WRD6 Trial

Strong adjacent 50 m by 50 m 1.6 m and 2.1 m cover designs with matching upper and compacted layers and long-term monitoring. Blocked by exact surveyed trial boundaries and numerical construction uncertainty.

### Faro Mine Pilot

Fifteen panels with GPS-controlled grading and strong construction quality procedures. Public records do not expose the exact matched-panel matrix, coordinate-tied panel boundaries, final measured thicknesses or numerical uncertainty.

## Next action

Continue only with candidates that can plausibly publish all of the following:

1. full-scale clean vegetated zones at least 30–40 m wide;
2. two measured final as-built depths or one measured depth plus a confirmed control;
3. coordinate-tied polygons;
4. matching radar-facing construction;
5. numerical construction uncertainty;
6. stable Sentinel-1 observation history.

Do not run Earth Engine or create calibration rows until a candidate passes the documentary gate.
