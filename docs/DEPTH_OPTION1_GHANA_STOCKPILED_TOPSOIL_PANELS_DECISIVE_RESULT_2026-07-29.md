# Option 1 decisive result — Ghana stockpiled-topsoil waste-rock-dump panels

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the public paper proves

The study describes a large operational mine-reclamation experiment in Ghana:

- one approximately 5.6-hectare waste-rock dump was divided into four approximately 1.4-hectare panels;
- each panel received a total reconstructed-soil profile of approximately 1 m;
- the four topsoil/subsoil combinations were 0/100 cm, 20/80 cm, 30/70 cm and 40/60 cm;
- the subsoil material is described as saprolite;
- the 40/60 cm profile is described as the Ghana EPA reclamation standard;
- plant diversity and tree growth were compared after six months, one year and four years;
- the 30/70 cm and 40/60 cm profiles produced broadly similar ecological results.

The experiment therefore passes the cheap total-area and same-general-soil-system screens in principle.

## Fatal blockers

The paper does not provide the execution evidence required by Option 1:

- the mine and exact study location are not identified;
- no latitude/longitude, surveyed corners, coordinate grid, GIS/CAD polygons or datum are published;
- the shapes and minimum widths of the four 1.4-hectare panels are not published, so a conservative 30–40 m clean interior cannot be verified;
- the 20/80, 30/70 and 40/60 cm values are treatment specifications, not certified final measured as-built layer depths;
- no numerical construction or survey uncertainty is published;
- no construction-completion map, repair map or instrumentation-exclusion map is provided;
- the ecological analysis combines soil profile with tree age, and the public record does not establish an unchanged, plot-specific Sentinel-1 interval after 2014.

Without a named site and official geometry, the candidate cannot be located or executed. Assigning exact panel depths would also overstate the public construction evidence.

## Reopening condition

Reopen only if an operator, regulator or author supplies:

1. the mine identity and exact site location;
2. coordinate-controlled panel boundaries and minimum widths;
3. measured final topsoil and saprolite thicknesses with uncertainty; and
4. mapped post-construction repairs and a stable Sentinel-1 observation interval.
