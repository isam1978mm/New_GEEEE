## Candidate 1 — Olympic View Sanitary Landfill, Washington

### Cheap-screen result

**ADVANCE TO DECISIVE DOCUMENT REVIEW.**

### Why it survives

- Washington Ecology describes an approximately 65-acre closed municipal landfill divided into three large adjoining areas: Old Barney White Landfill, Phase I, and Phase II.
- Disposal operations ended and closure was completed around 2002.
- The site has a geomembrane cap over the landfill areas and long-term post-closure controls.
- Ecology’s 2021 periodic review states that the cleanup actions appear protective.
- The public document index includes a 2011 Final Cover Investigation and Erosion Repair Report, post-closure plans, periodic reviews, and annual monitoring reports through 2024.
- The three large cover areas may contain different documented cover configurations while remaining broad enough for clean radar interiors.

### Decisive questions

1. Do the three landfill areas have different final cover depths or only different materials/design details?
2. Are any depth values final measured as-built thicknesses rather than design specifications?
3. Are measurements tied to exact polygons, survey grids, final contours, or test locations that support broad zones?
4. Do at least two zones share the same topsoil and vegetation assembly?
5. Is numerical survey or thickness uncertainty stated?
6. Did the 2011 investigation and repairs leave at least two zones unchanged through a defensible Sentinel-1 period?
7. Can each accepted zone retain at least 30–40 m clean interior after excluding slopes, roads, drainage, gas infrastructure, and repaired areas?

### Next record set

Review only the decisive official records:

- 2011 Final Cover Investigation and Erosion Repair Report;
- 2012 Post-Closure Operation and Maintenance Plan;
- 2017 Periodic Review;
- 2021 Periodic Review and Cleanup Action Plan Addendum;
- closure/CQA or as-built records for Old Barney White, Phase I, and Phase II when electronically available.

Do not run Earth Engine unless every documentary gate passes.

## Candidate 2 — Central Maui Landfill Phases I and II Closure, Hawaii

### Cheap-screen result

**NOT ADVANCED.**

### Positive evidence

- Hawaii Department of Health publishes a Construction Quality Assurance Report for the Phases I and II closure and states that operations-plan materials include as-built and record drawings.
- The two phases span approximately 17 acres and closure work was substantially complete by 2006–2007.

### Fatal blocker

Phase II has undergone later slope reconstruction and cover-system work, including Stage 3 and Stage 4 slope improvements published in 2022–2024 and additional Stage 6/7 slope improvements bid in 2026. The historical Phase I/II closure therefore does not provide a clean, stable two-zone Sentinel-1 comparison under the locked plan.

The route may be reopened only if exact official records prove two broad unaffected zones with final measured depths and an unchanged observation period outside all later work.
