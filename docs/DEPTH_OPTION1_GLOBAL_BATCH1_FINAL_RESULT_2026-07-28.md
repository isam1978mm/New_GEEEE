## Olympic View decisive result

### Official records reviewed

The Washington Department of Ecology site record confirms:

- an approximately 65-acre closed landfill;
- three adjoining landfill areas: Old Barney White Landfill, Phase I, and Phase II;
- closure around 2002–2003;
- a geomembrane cap and long-term post-closure monitoring;
- official periodic reviews and annual monitoring records;
- a public 2012 Post-Closure Operations and Maintenance Plan;
- references to final-cover CQA reports for different construction phases.

The 2012 post-closure plan provides the decisive cover profiles.

### Old Barney White Landfill

The record states that the original cover contained:

- 12 inches of low-permeability silt;
- 2 feet of native soil and topsoil.

It was later upgraded with:

- a 50-mil VLDPE geomembrane;
- a geocomposite drainage layer;
- topsoil.

### Phase I and Phase II

The record gives one repeated final-cover profile for both phases:

- 12 inches of vegetative topsoil and cover soil;
- geotextile fabric;
- 12-inch drainage layer;
- geonet composite;
- 40-mil geomembrane;
- 6-inch low-permeability soil.

### Fatal blockers

1. **Phase I and Phase II do not provide two depth conditions.** They share one stated cover profile.
2. **Old Barney White is not a matching-surface comparison.** Its cover assembly and construction history differ materially from the Phase I/II profile.
3. **The stated thicknesses are design descriptions.** The reviewed public record does not provide final measured as-built depth polygons with numerical uncertainty.
4. **No exact confirmed negative zone is provided.** A visually similar no-target area is not independently established.
5. **Maintenance and repair areas require exclusion.** The site includes cover inspections, repairs, drainage structures, gas infrastructure, settlement-response procedures, and repaired areas that would need exact mapping before radar use.
