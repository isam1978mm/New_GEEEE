# Option 1 — Global Depth — Batch 4 cheap screen — 2026-07-28

## Decision

**No candidate passed the complete documentary gate.**

Five new official-record candidates were screened before any radar work.

## Locked gates

A candidate may advance only when the public record supports:

- completed construction before a usable Sentinel-1 period;
- full-scale vegetated areas with at least 30–40 m clean interior after exclusions;
- two distinct final measured depth conditions, or one measured condition plus a confirmed control;
- coordinate-tied geometry;
- comparable radar-facing surfaces;
- numerical depth or survey uncertainty;
- physical stability during the observation period.

## Candidates

### 1. Coker's Sanitation Service Landfills, Delaware

Official sources show two landfills, both closed with multi-layer RCRA-type caps. Construction was completed in 1993 and EPA's 2024 five-year review found the remedy protective.

Cheap-screen result: **stop**.

Fatal blocker: the reviewed record applies the same multi-layer cap remedy to both landfills and does not establish two different final measured depth polygons or a confirmed control.

Sources:

- https://cumulis.epa.gov/supercpad/SiteProfiles/index.cfm?fuseaction=second.cleanup&id=0300097
- https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=91002ZYZ.TXT

### 2. Limestone Road, Maryland

Official sources show two landfills on opposite sides of Limestone Road. Both were capped; operation and maintenance continue. EPA records also document erosion and repair concerns at both caps.

Cheap-screen result: **stop**.

Fatal blocker: the reviewed public material does not establish two distinct final measured depth conditions, numerical uncertainty, or a confirmed control. Repair history also complicates a clean unchanged observation period.

Sources:

- https://cumulis.epa.gov/supercpad/SiteProfiles/index.cfm?fuseaction=second.cleanup&id=0300292
- https://cumulis.epa.gov/supercpad/SiteProfiles/index.cfm?fuseaction=second.schedule&id=0300292

### 3. Newport Dump, Kentucky

Official sources show one clay landfill cap, seeded with grass and maintained through long-term review.

Cheap-screen result: **stop**.

Fatal blocker: one cap condition only. No second measured depth area or confirmed control is published.

Sources:

- https://cumulis.epa.gov/supercpad/SiteProfiles/index.cfm?fuseaction=second.cleanup&id=0405547
- https://semspub.epa.gov/work/04/11174362.pdf

### 4. Sinclair Refinery, New York

EPA records show wastes from the South Landfill Area were excavated and consolidated into the Central Elevated Landfill Area, which was then capped.

Cheap-screen result: **stop**.

Fatal blocker: consolidation produced one capped disposal area, not two independent measured depth conditions or a measured condition plus control.

Sources:

- https://cumulis.epa.gov/supercpad/SiteProfiles/index.cfm?fuseaction=second.cleanup&id=0202093
- https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=91000FBM.TXT

### 5. Mather Air Force Base Landfill OU, California

Official records show two capped landfills: Site 3 and Site 4. Site 4 also received consolidated material from other landfill and soil sites. A Final Closure Certification Report is referenced in the 1998 Basewide ROD.

Cheap-screen result: **advance to decisive review**.

Reason: this is the only Batch 4 candidate with two large capped areas, long management, and a specifically referenced closure-certification package.
