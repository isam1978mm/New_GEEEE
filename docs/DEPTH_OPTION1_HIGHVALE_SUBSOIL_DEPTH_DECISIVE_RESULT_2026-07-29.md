# Option 1 decisive result — Highvale subsoil-depth experiment

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the public technical record proves

The Highvale Soil Reconstruction Reclamation Research Program is a genuine same-material depth experiment:

- experimental plots were established in 1982 at Highvale Mine;
- the subsoil-depth experiment used six treatment labels: 0, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00, 1.50 and 3.00 m;
- the six depths were randomized in main plots with two crop subplots, forage and cereal;
- the design used three replicated blocks;
- a common reconstructed-soil concept was used over sodic mine spoil;
- baseline soil/spoil characterization and intensive moisture, density, chemistry and crop monitoring were performed;
- 108 neutron-access-tube locations were monitored across the broader research program;
- two soil-sampling locations were selected in each subsoil subplot, with additional samples where soil-depth anomalies occurred;
- sampling crossed the topsoil/subsoil and subsoil/spoil contacts.

The experiment therefore passes the scientific-design screen for a replicated same-material depth comparison.

## Fatal blockers

The public record does not provide the ground-truth package required by Option 1:

- the 0–3 m values are treatment labels, not a published certified as-built thickness surface;
- local soil-depth anomalies were explicitly encountered, showing that the labels cannot safely be assigned uniformly to every point in a main plot;
- no numerical construction-thickness or survey uncertainty is attached to the treatment labels;
- no final same-coordinate before/after survey grid or measured plot-wide interface table is published;
- the randomized main plots are divided into forage and cereal subplots and contain monitoring and sampling infrastructure;
- the available layout indicates an experimental compound approximately 230–280 m across with repeated treatment subdivisions and a 20 m layout dimension, but it does not establish a conservative 30–40 m clean interior carrying one uniform depth label after crop, boundary and instrument exclusions;
- no official coordinate-tied treatment polygons, surveyed corners, GIS/CAD geometry or execution datum are published;
- the primary report covers the first monitoring year, and the public record does not prove that the exact treatment interiors remained unchanged and repair-free during a usable Sentinel-1 period beginning in 2014.

Assigning a radar polygon an exact treatment depth would therefore overstate what the public construction and geometry evidence supports.

## Reopening condition

Reopen only if an official or operator-issued package supplies:

1. final measured treatment thicknesses or surfaces with numerical uncertainty;
2. coordinate-controlled treatment boundaries and datum;
3. conservative clean interiors after crop, instrument, road and boundary exclusions; and
4. mapped post-2014 land use, repairs and stability for the exact plots.
