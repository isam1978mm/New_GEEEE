# Option 1 bounded result — Illinois Norris and Sunspot reconstructed-soil experiments

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## Norris Mine

The western Illinois Norris experiment evaluated topsoil replacement over reclaimed mine spoil.

- A-horizon material was placed over wheel spoil in a continuous wedge ranging from approximately 0 to 61 cm thickness.
- Crop response was evaluated over multiple years.
- The experiment was designed to study agricultural productivity across a continuous depth gradient.

The continuous wedge does not provide two broad official polygons carrying uniform measured depth labels. Selecting shallow and deep regions from the gradient would require analyst-defined thresholds and interpolated boundaries. The public summaries also do not provide certified final thickness surfaces, numerical construction uncertainty, coordinate-controlled treatment polygons, or post-2014 stability for exact zones.

## Sunspot Mine

The Sunspot experiment compared different reconstructed-soil architectures:

- topsoil over replaced B horizon;
- topsoil directly over dragline spoil;
- B horizon directly over dragline spoil; and
- dragline spoil only.

The surface and underlying rooting-media architecture therefore change between treatments. A radar difference could not be assigned to physical depth alone.
