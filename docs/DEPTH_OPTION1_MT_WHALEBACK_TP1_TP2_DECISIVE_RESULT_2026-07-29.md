# Option 1 decisive result — Mt Whaleback TP#1 and TP#2

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the public technical record proves

The Mt Whaleback cover-system program provides an unusually strong experimental comparison:

- two approximately 1-hectare horizontal field plots were constructed in early 1997 on the W22 overburden storage area;
- TP#1 used one operational lift of run-of-mine Banded Iron Formation cover material, described as approximately 2 m thick;
- TP#2 used two operational lifts of the same material, described as approximately 4 m thick;
- both plots were left bare, with no topsoil and no seeding;
- both retained the same block-dumped hummocky surface treatment;
- both contained large-scale lysimeters and instrumented moisture profiles;
- plot-specific monitoring continued for twelve years through September 2009;
- the 2 m and 4 m plots remained physically usable during that monitoring period.

The comparison therefore passes the cheap size, material, surface-treatment, and pre-2010 stability screens.

## Fatal blockers

The public papers describe operational target thicknesses, not a final measured calibration package.

They do not publish:

- a surveyed final thickness grid or before-and-after elevation table for TP#1 and TP#2;
- numerical construction or survey uncertainty for the 2 m and 4 m labels;
- official coordinate-tied plot corners, GIS polygons, CAD geometry, or a stated survey datum;
- a plot-specific monitoring or repair record after September 2009;
- proof that both exact 1-hectare interiors remained unchanged during a usable Sentinel-1 period beginning in 2014.

The plots were created by operational haul-truck lifts on an intentionally unlevelled paddock-dumped surface. Treating every point in each hectare as exactly 2.0 m or 4.0 m would therefore overstate what the public record measures.

A current 2024 Mt Whaleback field program concerns new, separate plots and does not supply continuity evidence for the original W22 TP#1 and TP#2 plots.

Reopen only if an official or operator-issued record supplies:

1. surveyed final TP#1 and TP#2 thicknesses with numerical uncertainty;
2. coordinate-controlled plot polygons and datum;
3. mapped repairs or disturbances; and
4. proof of a stable post-2014 interval for both exact plot interiors.
