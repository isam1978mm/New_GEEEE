# Option 1 decisive result — North Antelope/Rochelle topsoil-depth experiment

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the public technical record proves

The North Antelope/Rochelle Mine study is an unusually clean scientific depth experiment:

- the study was developed in 1998–1999 at the North Antelope/Rochelle Mine in northeastern Wyoming;
- three topsoil-replacement treatments were used: 15 cm, 30 cm and 56 cm;
- each treatment was replicated three times;
- the treatment plots were constructed on one uniform reclaimed site;
- slope, aspect, stockpiled-topsoil source, subsoil and backfill sources, and seed mix were intentionally controlled;
- the study location is published at approximately 43°32′15″ N, 105°15′19″ W;
- soil and vegetation monitoring was conducted from 2000 through 2004.

The candidate therefore passes the cheap same-material, same-surface and replicated-design screens.

## Fatal blockers

The public record does not provide the execution evidence required by Option 1:

- no plot dimensions are published in the accessible papers or indexed study descriptions;
- a conservative 30–40 m clean interior cannot therefore be established for any one treatment plot;
- no certified final as-built thickness grid or same-coordinate before/after survey is published;
- no numerical construction or survey uncertainty is attached to the 15, 30 and 56 cm labels;
- the published site coordinate locates the overall study area, not the boundaries or surveyed corners of the nine treatment plots;
- no official GIS/CAD treatment polygons or execution datum are published;
- the last plot-specific monitoring described in the public record ends in 2004;
- no public record proves that the exact treatment interiors remained identifiable, unchanged and repair-free during a usable Sentinel-1 period beginning in 2014.

A well-controlled scientific treatment label does not by itself provide a safe polygon-level physical-depth label.

## Reopening condition

Reopen only if an official or operator-issued package supplies:

1. treatment-plot dimensions and coordinate-controlled boundaries;
2. measured final thicknesses with numerical construction uncertainty;
3. conservative clean interiors after roads, instruments and boundaries are excluded; and
4. mapped post-2014 land-use, repair and stability records for the exact plots.
