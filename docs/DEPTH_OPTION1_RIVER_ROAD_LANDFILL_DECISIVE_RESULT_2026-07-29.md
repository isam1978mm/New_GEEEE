# Option 1 decisive result — River Road Landfill

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the official record proves

The recovered EPA administrative-record package confirms:

- River Road Landfill covers approximately 37.5 disposal acres;
- closure and final-cover construction were completed in 1986–1987;
- one minimum three-foot final soil cover was specified across the landfill;
- the final cover used on-site borrow material meeting Pennsylvania soil-texture requirements;
- 129 cover-certification pits were excavated, approximately two pits per acre;
- surveying support was provided during closure;
- the certification narrative says Sheet 1 of 3 mapped the surveyed pit locations;
- the certification narrative says Appendix A field reports listed final cover thickness for each pit;
- deficient areas were corrected and re-certified using overlapping pits and/or visual inspection;
- the final cover was revegetated, inspected, and maintained under the post-closure program.

## Fatal blockers

The scanned official public package does not include the decisive attachments.

- After the post-closure-plan text, the scan proceeds directly to Appendix C.
- The referenced Appendix A field reports are absent.
- The referenced Appendix B soil-analysis reports are absent.
- Sheet 1 of 3, the final-cover certification map, is not available in the recovered scan.
- Therefore, no pit-by-pit final thickness values or official coordinate-tied pit map can be extracted from the public package.

The certification also describes one corrected three-foot minimum cover design, not intentionally constructed shallow and deep treatments. Any depth zones inferred from construction deviations would be analyst-created rather than official labels, and deficient areas were corrected before certification.

The post-closure record requires continued inspection, reseeding, erosion control, and maintenance. It does not provide plot-specific post-2014 stability for any hypothetical derived zones.
