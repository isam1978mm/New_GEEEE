# Option 1 decisive result — Salzburg Landfill Cells 20–22

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the official public records prove

The recovered Michigan EGLE package confirms:

- Cells 20–22 were closed with waste in place effective August 2, 2017;
- the closed unit covers approximately 7.2 acres;
- the final cover design is common across the cells;
- the specified upper soil assembly is 6 inches of topsoil over 18 inches of protective soil;
- construction surveying was required as part of the CQA program;
- survey instruments were required to read to 0.01 foot;
- a vertical construction tolerance of approximately 0.1 foot was specified for the top of the foundation, protective-cover, and topsoil layers;
- as-built component thickness was supposed to be calculated by comparing surveys of the underlying surface and the completed component;
- survey locations were supposed to use a maximum typical spacing of 100 feet and, where possible, the same coordinates as the underlying survey.

## Fatal blockers

The public package recovered the 2016 design and CQA requirements, but not the final 2017 construction-certification dataset promised by those requirements.

The available public documents do not provide:

- the final same-coordinate before/after survey table;
- measured final protective-soil or topsoil thickness values;
- final coordinate-tied depth contours or polygons;
- two broad named zones with different measured final depths;
- numerical uncertainty attached to any final polygon-level depth;
- a mapped, repair-free Sentinel-1 observation interval for exact proposed zones.

The three cells use one repeated final-cover design. Even if the missing survey table showed construction variation, converting scattered deviations into shallow/deep polygons would require analyst-derived interpolation unless the certification itself defined those zones.

## Reopening condition

Reopen only if the official 2017 closure certification or CQA report becomes public and includes:

1. the final same-coordinate thickness survey table;
2. coordinate-controlled final drawings;
3. broad defensible depth zones rather than isolated measurements; and
4. post-closure repair and stability records for the exact zones.
