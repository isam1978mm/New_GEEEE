# Option 1 decisive result — Syncrude SW30-Dump D1 versus D3

Date: 2026-07-29

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO for numerical-depth calibration.**

This is **Option 1 evidence research**. Option 3 is not active.

## What the public technical record proves

The Southwest 30 / South Bison Hill program provides a strong full-scale comparison:

- three adjacent prototype covers were constructed on the north-facing slope in 1999;
- each plot is approximately one hectare and about 50 m wide by 150–200 m long;
- D1 and D3 share the same general slope position and cover-material system;
- D1 uses approximately 20 cm of peat-mineral mix over 30 cm of secondary till, for a nominal 0.50 m total cover;
- D3 uses approximately 20 cm of peat-mineral mix over 80 cm of secondary till, for a nominal 1.00 m total cover;
- both were seeded and planted under the same reclamation program;
- both were instrumented and monitored through a long-running research program;
- detailed soil profiles were sampled at ten locations along each cover transect in 10 cm increments through the cover-overburden interface;
- a 2012 Syncrude synthesis field program included the original capping-study area and concluded that a 50 cm cover had supported comparable forest capability to that date;
- later primary research continued to identify the D1/D2/D3 research watersheds and South Bison Hill as an active research landform.

The D1-versus-D3 pair therefore passes the cheap size, upper-surface, material-family, slope, and vegetation comparability screens.

## Fatal blockers

The public record does not provide the final ground-truth package required by Option 1.

- The 0.50 m and 1.00 m values are repeatedly described as design or nominal cover thicknesses.
- No certified as-built survey, before-and-after elevation grid, or plot-wide measured thickness table is published.
- The ten soil profiles provide local interface observations, but the accessible records do not publish a complete table of measured interface depths with horizontal coordinates and uncertainty.
- No numerical construction-thickness or survey uncertainty is attached to D1 and D3.
- Published figures locate the plots schematically within South Bison Hill, but do not provide official coordinate-tied corners, GIS polygons, CAD geometry, or a survey datum for execution.
- Later studies confirm continued research at South Bison Hill, but do not prove that both exact D1 and D3 clean interiors remained unchanged, repair-free, and free of instrumentation disturbance throughout a usable Sentinel-1 period beginning in 2014.

Assigning every pixel in D1 to exactly 0.50 m and every pixel in D3 to exactly 1.00 m would overstate what the public measurements support. Deriving execution polygons from published figures would also create analyst-derived geometry rather than official ground truth.

## Reopening condition

Reopen only if an official or operator-issued package supplies:

1. measured final D1 and D3 thickness distributions or surfaces;
2. numerical construction or survey uncertainty;
3. coordinate-controlled plot boundaries and datum; and
4. mapped post-2014 repairs, instruments, and a stable observation interval for both exact interiors.
