# Former Photech Imaging Site — Redevelopment Follow-up

Date: 2026-07-25

## Decision

**Status:** closed as a numerical-depth calibration candidate.

Photech has strong remedial documentation, mapped excavation areas, recorded excavation depths, closure sampling, and a final grading/infrastructure plan. It fails the unchanged-surface requirement because the property was prepared for roads and buildings and later received two occupied buildings.

## What is confirmed

- Site: Former Photech Imaging Site, 1000 Driving Park Avenue, Rochester, New York; NYSDEC ERP Site B00016.
- Operations ended and the facility was abandoned in 1991.
- Fifteen former buildings were demolished in 2010, including below-grade tunnels.
- Contaminated soil was excavated from multiple areas of concern and disposed off-site.
- AOC 3A, the former retention pond/burn-pit area, had approximately 3,467 tons of soil removed.
- AOC 3A closure samples document excavation depths generally between 3 and 5 feet below grade.
- AOC 14 excavation depths ranged from approximately 4 to 11 feet below grade.
- The Final Engineering Report includes figures for excavation limits, closure samples, and excavation depths.
- The site was backfilled and graded. Structural fill was compacted to a 95 percent standard in planned roadway and building areas.
- New stormwater infrastructure was installed and disturbed areas were hydroseeded.
- Long-term groundwater monitoring and annual cover/property-use inspections continued.
- The 2024 Periodic Review Report states that two new buildings were present and both required operational sub-slab depressurization systems.

## Why it is not usable

1. The site was intentionally prepared for future commercial development.
2. Two new buildings were constructed before the January 2024 inspection.
3. Roads, utilities, drainage structures, compacted structural fill, monitoring wells, and cover maintenance alter the radar response.
4. The current public site map labels the Farmer John Popcorn Building and LaserShip Building.
5. The exact excavation figures and grading-plan PDF could not be rendered in the current environment, so no survey-grade polygon or numerical survey accuracy could be independently extracted.
6. No unchanged Sentinel-1 comparison period is proven for an isolated excavation cell.
