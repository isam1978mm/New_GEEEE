## Exact public-record failure

The searchable EPA package contains:

- the closure-certification narrative;
- the table of contents naming Appendix A Field Reports, Appendix B Soil Analysis Reports, and Plan Sheet 1 Final Cover Certification;
- a soil-classification table grouped by pit identifiers.

It does **not** contain:

- the Appendix A field-report pages with individual final pit thicknesses;
- the Appendix B soil-analysis attachment;
- Final Cover Certification Sheet 1 showing surveyed pit locations;
- surveyed pit coordinates;
- numerical survey-position accuracy;
- numerical depth-measurement uncertainty.

The available administrative-record page sequence advances from AR304845 directly to AR304847, where Appendix C begins. Because Appendix A and Appendix B would require many pages, this strongly indicates that the required attachments were omitted from the electronically available bundle rather than merely lost through poor text recognition.

The visible pit table is not a depth table. It reports soil texture/classification categories, not final cover thicknesses.

## Why no calibration geometry can be created

Without the omitted attachments, it is impossible to defensibly:

1. distinguish initial deficient readings from final corrected readings;
2. assign a final measured depth to a real surveyed pit location;
3. identify two large zones with non-overlapping final depth ranges;
4. calculate boundary and depth uncertainty;
5. test clean 20 m Sentinel-1 pixel support.

The known 3-foot value is a certified minimum for the completed cap, not evidence that two named areas have different measured final depths.
