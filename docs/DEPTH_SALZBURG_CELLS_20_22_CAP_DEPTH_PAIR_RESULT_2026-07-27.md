# Salzburg Landfill Cells 20-22 cap-depth result - 2026-07-27

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

Salzburg is a strong documentation near-miss. The public records prove a recent vegetated closure, an explicit same-coordinate thickness-survey method, and numerical survey accuracy. They do not publish the completed 2017 thickness measurements required to form two broad depth zones.

## What passed

- Cells 20-22 closed in 2017.
- The cap covers approximately four acres.
- Postclosure maintenance and cap-elevation surveys support a plausible stable Sentinel-1 period.
- The CQA plan requires as-built thickness to be calculated by comparing surveys of underlying and overlying surfaces at the same coordinates.
- Typical survey spacing is no more than 100 feet, with additional observations at toes, slope midpoints, slope tops and grade breaks.
- Survey instruments were required to read to 0.01 foot, with a stated 0.1-foot vertical construction tolerance.
- The upper construction is one common vegetated profile across Cells 20-22.

## Fatal blocker

The recovered official package does not contain the completed Cells 20-22 Construction Certification Report, record drawings or measured thickness table.

The public design specifies one uniform profile across all three cells:

- 6-inch topsoil layer;
- 18-inch protective soil layer;
- geocomposite drainage layer;
- 40-mil geomembrane;
- two geosynthetic clay liner layers;
- 12-inch foundation soil layer.

The survey requirements prove how final thickness was supposed to be measured. They do not provide the final measured values or two broad measured depth polygons.

## Cells 17-19 rescue check

The same official package includes a regulator letter approving the older Cells 17-19 closure and stating that the cap remained in good condition. It does not include the underlying closure-certification measurements or a coordinate-tied thickness grid.

Therefore Cells 17-19 cannot serve as the exact second depth condition. The records also do not prove that its complete radar-facing upper assembly is equivalent to the 2017 Cells 20-22 surface.
