# Sconondoa Radar-Linkage Execution Status — 2026-07-26

**Branch:** `main`  
**Status:** substitute site screen inconclusive at visible as-built geometry gate

## Evidence confirmed

The official Final Engineering Report confirms:

- remediation was completed in three phases between January 2008 and December 2012;
- Thew Associates PE-LS, PLLC performed construction surveying;
- horizontal and vertical survey control was maintained during excavation, backfilling and restoration;
- post-construction surveys documented final as-built topographic conditions;
- Appendix B contains the as-built survey drawings;
- individual remedial cells had materially different excavation depths;
- several cells were restored with the same 8-inch compacted subbase and 4-inch crushed-stone surface profile;
- the report contains finite examples of within-cell depth variation, including Cell 9A from approximately six inches to six feet below existing grade;
- some reuse material was placed only at depths greater than six feet below ground surface.

This is the strongest of the four screened sites for a genuine within-site depth-ordering experiment.

## Geometry and source-access result

The 90 MB public appendix containing the property survey map, as-built survey drawings and project photographs could not be rendered or downloaded through the available routes.

The main Final Engineering Report was readable as parsed text and confirms the existence and purpose of the as-built drawings. Its final figure pages could not be visually rendered. No unseen survey sheet or figure was claimed as reviewed.

Cell names, depth descriptions and surface-restoration text are not enough to construct private polygons. The radar runner requires exact or visibly reviewable target zones that can exclude:

- the Service Center Building;
- the active gas regulator station;
- asphalt, sidewalks and utilities;
- monitoring and recovery wells;
- the Tailrace and drainage structures;
- mixed grass, riprap and other different surface treatments.

## Depth-ordering decision

The source supports a credible physical depth ordering in principle, and multiple cells share similar gravel restoration. However, no cell polygon can be matched to the documented depth values without the as-built survey appendix.

The report's broad statement that excavation depths ranged from approximately 5 to 20 feet cannot be assigned to radar pixels. The `greater than six feet` reuse-material condition is also a lower bound rather than a finite point label.
