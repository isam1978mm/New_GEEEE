# St. Louis Airport Site measured-depth result - 2026-07-27

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

SLAPS is a large, well-documented excavation project, but its public records do not provide two final measured as-built depth polygons suitable for the approved numerical-depth calibration plan.

## What passed

- The site covers approximately 21.7 acres.
- More than 600,000 cubic yards were removed over multiple excavation phases.
- Formal closure occurred in May 2007.
- USACE states that a final Post-Remedial Action Report and Final Status Survey Evaluation was completed in May 2009.
- The phase packages include coordinate-controlled survey units, excavation contour maps, cross-sections and detailed excavation-depth drawings.
- The design calculations used point and contour data to build a triangulated irregular network and a 3-foot calculation grid.

## Fatal blocker

The available excavation depths are **planned or predicted cut surfaces**, not completed final measured depths.

The Phase 4-5 work description states that:

- excavation contours were derived from historical and pre-design contamination data;
- technicians would guide excavation until radiological criteria were met;
- the initial gross cut lines could change during implementation;
- drawings would be adjusted when additional field information required it.

Therefore the design contours cannot be treated as final as-built excavation depths.

## Final-status survey limitation

The final-status survey plan verifies whether residual radiological concentrations meet cleanup criteria. It uses statistical survey units, triangular sample grids and laboratory analysis.

It does not provide:

- a final before-and-after topographic depth table;
- final measured excavation-depth polygons;
- final measured backfill thickness;
- project-specific horizontal or vertical uncertainty for physical depth measurements.

The plan says excavation stops when radiological surveys indicate that the bottom meets cleanup criteria. It then checks residual contamination. That is different from measuring the physical depth of the completed excavation.

## Missing final report

The USACE site page and reading room confirm that the final Post-Remedial Action Report/Final Status Survey Evaluation was completed in May 2009. The report is not exposed as a normal indexed PDF in the public administrative-record list and could not be recovered from the official endpoint during this screen.

The absence matters because the earlier phase documents explicitly allow the excavation geometry to change during construction.

## Surface and geometry blockers

The site includes or retained substantial infrastructure and non-comparable terrain:

- an active rail spur;
- Coldwater Creek and reconstructed channel areas;
- drainage ditches;
- sedimentation and retention basins;
- berms and steep excavation slopes;
- monitoring wells and utilities.

The available records do not establish two final measured zones that each retain a 30-40 m clean interior after excluding those features.

They also do not prove that two different measured excavation zones received one identical final topsoil and vegetation assembly.
