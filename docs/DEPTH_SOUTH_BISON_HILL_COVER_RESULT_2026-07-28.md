# South Bison Hill cover-depth screen result

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

South Bison Hill has three large, long-monitored vegetated cover plots, but the public record does not provide final measured numerical depth labels or coordinate-controlled calibration polygons.

## What the public record proves

- Three adjacent one-hectare plots were constructed in 1999.
- The plots are approximately 50 m wide and extend along a north-facing approximately 5:1 slope.
- The cover prescriptions are:
  - D1: 20 cm surface peat/glacial mix over 30 cm secondary subsoil, nominal total 50 cm;
  - D2: 15 cm topsoil over 20 cm secondary subsoil, nominal total 35 cm;
  - D3: 20 cm surface peat/glacial mix over 80 cm secondary subsoil, nominal total 100 cm.
- The 50 cm and 100 cm covers therefore share a nominal 20 cm surface layer.
- The plots were instrumented and monitored over many years.

## Fatal blockers

### 1. Depths are prescriptions, not final measured labels

The public papers describe the intended or nominal cover configurations. They do not publish:

- final measured total thickness values for each plot;
- an as-built thickness grid or before/after elevation table;
- numerical thickness uncertainty;
- numerical horizontal or vertical survey accuracy.

### 2. Exact public geometry is missing

No surveyed plot-corner northing/easting table or georeferenced GIS/CAD polygon deliverable was found.

Each approximately 50 m-wide strip contains substantial monitoring and drainage infrastructure, including:

- a midslope instrumentation station;
- neutron access tubes at top, middle and toe positions;
- interflow cutoffs and collection systems;
- runoff swales and weirs.

The public geometry is therefore insufficient to prove a 30-40 m clean interior after exclusions without inventing boundaries.

### 3. Surface matching is imperfect

The D3 surface layer contained unintended inclusions of secondary material and initially supported less established vegetation. Later vegetation became more comparable, but the public record does not define an exact common, undisturbed Sentinel-1 observation polygon for D1 and D3.

## Gate result

```text
two large exact final measured depth zones = no
coordinate-tied measured depth geometry = no
matching upper soil and vegetation = partially, but not cleanly proven
numerical depth uncertainty = no
30-40 m clean interior for each exact zone = not proven
stable Sentinel-1 period for exact zones = not proven
```
