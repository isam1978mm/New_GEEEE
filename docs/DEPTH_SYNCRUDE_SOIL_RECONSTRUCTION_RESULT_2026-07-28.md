# Syncrude Soil Reconstruction Project depth screen result

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

The project contains paired 20 cm and 40 cm soil-reconstruction treatments, but the individual plots are too narrow for the locked radar-footprint requirement.

## What the primary report proves

- The experiment occupied an approximately **7-hectare tailings-sand pad**.
- Nine combinations of peat, mineral overburden and tailings sand were tested.
- Each mixture was constructed at nominal depths of **20 cm and 40 cm**.
- The recovered primary report includes a Typical Plot Configuration drawing.
- That drawing labels a typical plot as approximately **22 m × 44 m**.

## Fatal blocker

The narrow plot dimension is only **22 m**.

The approved plan requires each candidate zone to retain at least **30-40 m of clean width after exclusions**. This plot fails before exclusions are applied.

The drawing also shows internal planting and sampling subdivisions. Plot edges, internal rows and any monitoring areas would reduce the usable width further.

Because the size gate is fatal, the review stopped without spending additional time on:

- as-built thickness measurements;
- numerical depth uncertainty;
- coordinate control;
- vegetation matching;
- Sentinel-1-era stability.
