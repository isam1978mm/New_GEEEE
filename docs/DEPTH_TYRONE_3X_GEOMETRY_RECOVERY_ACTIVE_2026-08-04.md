## Geometry blocker

The public PDF drawings show plot shapes, relative positions, acreage, test-pit symbols, instruments, lysimeters, profiles, berms and drainage features. They do not provide:

- plot-corner northings/eastings;
- latitude/longitude plot vertices;
- a stated horizontal datum or coordinate reference system;
- survey-control or benchmark coordinates;
- native CAD/GIS boundaries;
- GPS, CAES or survey-point exports;
- coordinate-tied masks for instrumentation, subsidence, ponding, fertilizer strips, berms or drains.

The plots are large enough in principle for conservative 30-40 m interiors, but those interiors cannot yet be located or defended on Sentinel-1 imagery.

## Stability blocker

The 2005 measurements predate Sentinel-1. Later records mention localized lysimeter subsidence, ponding, erosion monitoring, corrective work, instrumentation and vegetation development. A usable radar interval requires plot-specific evidence that the selected clean interiors were not regraded, repaired, trafficked or otherwise disturbed during the chosen Sentinel-1 period.

## No-wait technical fallback

A research-only georeferencing attempt may proceed in parallel, but it cannot create a calibration record unless it passes all of these gates:

1. Use an official later coordinate-controlled map of the Reclaimed 3X facility.
2. Identify at least six persistent, well-distributed control features visible on both the 2006 test-plot drawing and the coordinate-controlled map/orthophoto.
3. Reserve at least two additional features as independent check points.
4. Fit and report the transformation and residuals.
5. Require conservative independent check-point error below one-half Sentinel-1 pixel and preferably below 5 m.
6. Digitize TP5/TP6 boundaries and all exclusion features separately.
7. Treat the result as derived research geometry, not official survey geometry.
8. Do not create a calibration row until plot-specific post-2014 stability is also proven.

Failure to obtain enough persistent control features closes the derived-georeferencing route without weakening the gate.
