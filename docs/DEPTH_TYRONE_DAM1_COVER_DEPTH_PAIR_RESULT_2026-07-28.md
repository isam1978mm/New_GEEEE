# Tyrone Mine Dam 1 cover-depth screen result

## Decision

**NOT GOOD TO GO**

Tyrone Dam 1 is a large completed reclaimed area, but the public package does not provide two exact final measured depth zones that can be used for numerical depth calibration.

## What the official package proves

- The screened reclamation covers approximately **402 acres**.
- The public summary reports approximately **309 acres with cover greater than 3 feet**.
- The area is divided into Dam 1 top, Dam 1 outslope and the 12 Ponds top.
- The financial-assurance application references electronic application materials supplied on CD.

## Fatal blocker

The public record publishes only acreage classified as **greater than 3 feet**. It does not publish:

- the actual measured thickness values;
- an exact depth for the remaining acreage;
- a second exact shallow or deep condition;
- measured-thickness contours or point tables;
- coordinate-tied shallow/deep polygons;
- numerical depth or survey uncertainty.

A threshold category such as `greater than 3 feet` cannot be used as an exact numerical depth label.

The online Attachment I is only a placeholder PDF. The recovered five-document package contained zero embedded electronic files. Later financial-assurance packages concern other stockpiles and do not supply the missing Dam 1 thickness geometry.
