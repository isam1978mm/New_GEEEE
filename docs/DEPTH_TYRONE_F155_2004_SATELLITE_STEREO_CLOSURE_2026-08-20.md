# Tyrone depth — F155 2004 satellite-stereo closure — 2026-08-20

## Purpose

Record the accuracy-gate screen for historical high-resolution satellite stereo systems that were operational during the Sep-2004 to May-2005 construction window.

## Frozen gate

Historical surface acceptance requires `RMSEz <= 0.15 m` before any depth holdouts are used.

## Systems screened

### SPOT-5 HRS
Published performance is on the metre scale. A 2004 Reference3D description reports about 4 m RMS in-flight altimetric assessment, while later evaluations commonly report roughly 5–10 m vertical accuracy in medium/high relief.

### IKONOS stereo
Published studies commonly report vertical accuracy on the order of roughly 0.5–1.0 m or worse. One published checkpoint evaluation reports approximately 1.18 m vertical RMSE.

### QuickBird stereo
Published studies report vertical errors generally on the order of roughly 0.5–1.4 m. A highly corrected example reported about 0.29 m vertical error, still above the frozen 0.15 m RMSEz gate.

## Decision

**Historical IKONOS / QuickBird / SPOT-5 stereo is CLOSED as a Step-4 repair source.**

Reason: even favorable published vertical performance is materially worse than the frozen 0.15 m historical-surface RMSEz requirement. Archive coverage over Tyrone therefore does not need to be purchased or exhaustively searched for this numerical-depth method.

This closure is based on achievable vertical accuracy, not on absence of imagery.
