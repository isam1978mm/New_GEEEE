# Tyrone depth — F157 PDTI / 2004 aerial survey trail closure — 2026-08-20

## Purpose

Determine whether the known PDTI Engineering / D. Benavidez survey trail contains a second survey-grade Tyrone topographic or aerial survey after 3X grading began in September 2004 and before cover placement in May 2005.

## Relevant construction window

The admissible Step-4 target remains approximately:

- after 3X grading began in September 2004; and
- before cover placement began in May 2005.

The required source must represent the immediate post-grading / pre-cover surface and must ultimately satisfy the frozen historical-surface accuracy gate.

## Public survey/topography trail checked

Official Tyrone records state that:

- PDTI Engineering surveyed the mine at least annually beginning in 1995;
- aerial surveys were conducted from 2000 through 2004;
- the documented 1995–2004 mine-wide topographic package is PDTI Engineering AutoCAD data attributed to D. Benavidez and supplied May 3, 2004;
- those vectors were converted to TINs and 25 ft x 25 ft raster grids for topographic/volumetric analysis.

This May-3-2004 delivery is too early for the September-2004 grading event at 3X.

## 2004 aerial-flight dating clue

A separate official Tyrone work plan identifies its site background as a **February 2004 aerial photograph**.

Another official Tyrone study, dated March 2006, states that wind-blown tailing mapping was delineated on **1:6,000 scale aerial photographs from the 2004 flight of the Tyrone Mine**.

The public record therefore strongly ties the known 2004 mine aerial/topographic chain to early 2004, before 3X grading began. The second document does not itself print the month of the '2004 flight,' so the equivalence of these references is treated as a supported inference rather than an independently proven identity.

## Later 2005 references checked

The public 2005 PDTI/M3 references found are design, reclamation, and permit submittals, including:

- PDTI 2005b / M3 2005a — site-wide BER for Tailing Impoundments 3, 2, 1A, 1 and 1X;
- other PDTI 2005 permit/reclamation reports.

They do not expose a new mine-wide post-September-2004 survey of 3X. The site-wide BER also concerns other tailing impoundments, not a new 3X pre-cover survey surface.

## Search result

Targeted repository and public-web searches did **not** recover any official reference to a second PDTI / Benavidez mine-wide topographic survey or aerial flight acquired between September 2004 and May 2005.

This is a public-route conclusion. It does **not** prove that no internal company survey, construction GPS dataset, machine-control surface, or unpublished flight ever existed.

## Decision

**F157 — PUBLIC PDTI / BENAVIDEZ SECOND-SURVEY ROUTE CLOSED.**

The known survey-grade 2004 topographic chain is pre-grading and cannot serve as the missing immediate post-grading / pre-cover 3X surface.

Do not reuse the May-3-2004 topography as if it were post-grading.

Do not claim that all internal 2004–2005 survey records are nonexistent; only the public PDTI/Benavidez trail is closed.
