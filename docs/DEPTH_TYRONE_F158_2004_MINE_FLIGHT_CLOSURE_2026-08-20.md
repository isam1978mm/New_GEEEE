# Tyrone depth — F158 original 2004 mine-flight archive closure — 2026-08-20

## Purpose

Trace the original aerial-photography provenance behind official Tyrone references to a "2004 flight of the Tyrone Mine" and determine whether its original stereo photography could represent the missing immediate post-grading / pre-cover 3X surface.

## Official evidence

An official Tyrone Affected Areas Study Work Plan states that Figure 2 identifies mine facilities on a **recent (February 2004) aerial photograph**. The same figure includes Tailing No. 3X.

A later March 2006 official Golder report states that wind-blown tailing deposits were delineated on **1:6,000 scale aerial photographs from the 2004 flight of the Tyrone Mine**.

Separately, the documented 1995–2004 mine-wide topography is PDTI Engineering AutoCAD data attributed to D. Benavidez and supplied May 3, 2004, and the mine was surveyed at least annually with aerial surveys conducted from 2000 through 2004.

## Archive/provenance search

Targeted public searches were performed for:

- original stereo frames / diapositives / scans;
- flight index or photo-center maps;
- exact acquisition date beyond the explicit February-2004 reference;
- camera / calibration report;
- ground-control report;
- aerial-mapping contractor or vendor;
- orthophoto / photogrammetric deliverables;
- archive accession or catalog references.

No public contractor name, flight number, camera/calibration report, photo-center index, archive accession, or original stereo-frame catalog was recovered for the private Tyrone 2004 flight.

The official figure title block available in the indexed PDF identifies Daniel B. Stephens & Associates GIS production information but does not expose an aerial-photography vendor or flight identifier.

## Scientific decision

**F158 — ORIGINAL 2004 MINE-FLIGHT ROUTE CLOSED FOR STEP 4.**

The decisive issue is timing, not merely archive availability. The only explicit acquisition month recovered for the relevant mine aerial is **February 2004**, which predates the start of 3X reclamation grading in September 2004 by about seven months.

Therefore, even if the original stereo frames, camera calibration, and control survived, reconstructing that flight would represent pre-grading terrain and would not supply the required immediate post-grading / pre-cover surface.

Do not spend money or send a records request for the February-2004 flight for the Step-4 numerical-depth method.

This closure does not prove that no later private aerial flight ever occurred; it closes the specifically documented public 2004-flight chain traced here.

## Newly exposed record lead

During F158, the official October-2004 supplemental materials report exposed a distinct record type:

> PDTI "Pit maps," reflecting mine-wide changes in pits and stockpiles, were developed quarterly by PDTI since 1973.

The report says hard-copy pit maps for selected historical periods were supplied through Greystone/DBS&A and digitized with elevation attributes.

This record type was not found in the existing repo continuity searches under the phrase "quarterly pit maps" and must be screened separately rather than assumed equivalent to the May-3-2004 annual AutoCAD surface.
