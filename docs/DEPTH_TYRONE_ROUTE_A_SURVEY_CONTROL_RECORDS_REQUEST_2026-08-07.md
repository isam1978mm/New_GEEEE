# Tyrone Route A — Targeted Survey-Control Records Request

Date: 2026-08-07

## Purpose

This is a narrow follow-up request for the survey/control records needed to georeference the already documented 3X Tailing Reclamation Test Plots 5 and 6. It is not a request for another general Tyrone production.

## Request text

Please provide electronic copies of records concerning survey control, grade control, and as-built construction for the **3X Tailing Impoundment Reclamation** at the Tyrone Mine, Permit No. GR010RE, with priority on the following identified report:

> **M3 Engineering, 2008a — Construction Quality Assurance Report: Tailing Impoundment 3X Reclamation [Draft], prepared for Freeport McMoRan Copper and Gold, Tyrone Operations, June 2008.**

Official Tyrone Closure/Closeout Plan records state that the 3X facility Construction Quality Assurance Report was submitted to the agencies in August 2008. Please include any final, revised, agency-submitted, or complete version of that report in addition to the June 2008 draft.

Please include all appendices, plates, drawings, tables, exhibits, survey sheets, photographs, and electronic attachments associated with the report.

In particular, please provide any records containing or defining:

- survey benchmarks, monuments, control points, or project control used for the 3X reclamation;
- northing/easting or latitude/longitude coordinates;
- the coordinate reference system, State Plane/UTM/project grid definition, datum, units, basis of bearing, or coordinate transformation;
- surveyed coordinates or control for **3X Test Plot 5**, **3X Test Plot 6**, or the six lysimeters installed in the 3X test-plot program;
- conventional GPS survey data used for 3X construction, grade control, lysimeter locations, or as-built verification;
- CAES (Computer Aided Earth Moving System) / machine-control data, surfaces, point files, linework, or exports used for 3X grade control;
- native or electronic CAD/survey files underlying the 3X reclamation as-built drawings or Plate 1, including DWG, DXF, LandXML/XML, CSV/TXT point files, shapefiles, geodatabases, or equivalent retained formats;
- surveyor certifications, survey-control reports, as-built survey tables, coordinate lists, control diagrams, or field-survey deliverables.

If the agency does not retain the native electronic files, please provide the corresponding PDF/scanned survey sheets, plan sets, appendices, transmittals, or record indexes that identify where those files were delivered or archived.

If responsive records are maintained under a different title, project number, or document-control identifier, please include those records as well. The request is intended to capture the survey/control package for the 3X Tailing Impoundment reclamation and the records necessary to relate the local Test Plot 5/Test Plot 6 construction grid to a real-world coordinate system.

## Why this request is targeted

The official 3X as-built material already produced establishes measured Test Plot 5 and Test Plot 6 construction depths and local plot geometry. Official records also state that conventional GPS surveys and CAES were used during 3X construction. The remaining missing item is the numeric survey/control tie needed to transform the local plot geometry to global coordinates.

## Acceptance criterion after production

A production is potentially sufficient when it contains a defensible numeric relationship between the local 3X plot geometry and a stated survey/global coordinate system, together with CRS/datum/units metadata.

Do not treat a broad mine map, approximate aerial location, or unlabeled drawing as sufficient survey control.
