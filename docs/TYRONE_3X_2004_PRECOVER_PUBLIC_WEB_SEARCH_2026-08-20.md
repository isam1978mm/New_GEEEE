# Tyrone 3X 2004 pre-cover engineering surface - public-web search result - 2026-08-20

## Decision

**Under the current free / no-email / no-payment constraint, the public-web route for obtaining a usable 2004 pre-cover 3X engineering surface is exhausted for now.**

This is a data-access closure, not evidence that the engineering data never existed.

The public record now establishes that the relevant engineering and survey data trail did exist. However, the actual 2004 native pre-cover/grading surface, CAD/TIN/grid, or equivalent survey file has not been found as a public download.

General numerical depth estimation therefore remains blocked.

## What is proven to exist

### 1. June 2004 M3 Basic Engineering Report

The Tyrone Closure/Closeout Plan identifies:

> M3, 2004d. Basic Engineering Report, Tailing Impoundment #3X Reclamation, Volume 1 BER Summary Report. Prepared for Phelps Dodge Tyrone, Inc., Tyrone, New Mexico. June.

The same public closure record states that this BER was submitted to NMED and MMD in June 2004 before reclamation of 3X began.

This makes the BER the strongest known pre-reclamation engineering-document target.

### 2. June 2004 Tetra Tech cover-design report

The public 3X test-plot as-built report identifies:

> Tetra Tech. 2004. Cover design report: 3X tailings impoundment. Prepared for Phelps Dodge Tyrone, Inc. Tyrone, NM. June 2004.

This document also predates 3X reclamation and is relevant to the cover design.

### 3. August 2008 3X CQAR

Later Tyrone closure records state that the Construction Quality Assurance Report for the 3X facility was submitted in August 2008 and identify the reference as:

> M3, 2008a. Construction Quality Assurance Report: Tailing Impoundment 3X Reclamation [Draft]. Prepared for Freeport McMoRan Copper and Gold, Tyrone Operations. June 2008.

The public test-plot report had earlier stated that final construction details would ultimately be provided in the 3X construction-quality-assurance design/report package.

## Strong new evidence that precise digital grading/survey data existed

The publicly downloadable 3X test-plot as-built report describes the actual construction method used during reclamation:

- major slope regrading with D8R dozers;
- final grading with a 14H motor grader;
- **Computer Aided Earthmoving System (CAES)** used for grade control and equipment operations;
- the CAES process was complemented by **conventional GPS surveys**;
- cover was placed to the 3X design specifications.

This is important because it demonstrates that reclamation was not performed from approximate visual grading alone. A digital grade-control / survey workflow was used.

Therefore a high-value engineering data trail almost certainly existed during construction. The missing problem is access to the native or exported surface, not proof that 3X was surveyed or digitally controlled.

Do not overstate this evidence: the public report does not itself provide the complete native CAES terrain model, pre-cover TIN, CAD surface, or GPS point file needed by the depth experiment.

## Why this matters scientifically

The remaining direct elevation-difference route needs a verified surface representing the substrate immediately before cover placement, or another demonstrably equivalent pre-placement engineering surface.

A generic pre-2004 terrain model is not automatically sufficient because 3X reclamation included substantial top-surface and outslope grading before/with cover-subbase placement.

The public closure record explicitly describes 3X reclamation as including:

- outslope and top-surface grading;
- storm-water diversions and drainage construction;
- placement of suitable cover sub-base on top and outslope surfaces.

Therefore `final surface - old historical terrain` cannot safely be called cover thickness unless the chosen historical surface corresponds to the verified pre-cover substrate and grading confounds are controlled.

## What would reopen this route

Reopen only if a new file appears that provides one of the following for 3X:

- pre-cover / post-grading surface in CAD, TIN, grid, LandXML or equivalent form;
- CAES design/as-built terrain export;
- GPS survey point cloud / point list tied to the 3X control system;
- engineering drawing set containing sufficiently dense, spatially tied pre-cover substrate contours that can meet the frozen vertical-accuracy gate;
- another independently verifiable pre-placement surface with adequate uncertainty metadata.

Any such surface must still pass the existing frozen historical-surface accuracy and confound gates before measured cover depths are revealed for comparison.
