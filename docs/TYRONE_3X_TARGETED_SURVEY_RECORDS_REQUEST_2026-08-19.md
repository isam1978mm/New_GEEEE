## Do not broaden the request

Do not ask again for:

- general Tyrone mine records;
- more cover-depth reports;
- meteorological data;
- gradation reports;
- duplicate CQA PDFs already received;
- unrelated Tailing Dam 1 or stockpile records.

The scientific need is specifically the **3X pre-cover/subgrade elevation surface**, with the final/post-cover surface and datum/accuracy metadata if available.

## What happens after receipt

1. Preserve returned files exactly as received.
2. Identify file formats, coordinate system, vertical datum and survey accuracy.
3. Do **not** calculate depth immediately.
4. Preregister the co-registration and uncertainty gate before comparing derived depth with the known Tyrone measurements.
5. Only then compute `final surface - pre-cover surface` and validate against the six broad plots plus independent mapped test pits.

If the agency confirms that the original survey surfaces are unavailable, the next fallback is a separately preregistered historical stereo-aerial reconstruction study.
